🔒 PRIVACY POLICY

How we protect your family's data. Plain words, no legalese tricks.
Last updated: October 3, 2026
👨‍👩‍👧 Parents, start here: Fire Babys is made for kids, so we follow the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We collect as little as possible, we never sell data, we never show behavioral ads to children, and you can ask us to see or delete your child's information at any time by emailing firebabysofficial@gmail.com.

📋What we collect

  • Email address — when you sign in to play. Used only to recognize your account on your device.
  • Display name — the nickname you pick for multiplayer. It can be anything; we suggest not using a real name.
  • Gameplay data — scores, leaderboard entries, game progress, and settings. Stored so your game works.
  • Basic technical info — like device type and pages visited, collected automatically by our analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics, privacy-friendly, no cross-site tracking).

🎯How we use it

  • To let you sign in and keep your game progress.
  • To run multiplayer lobbies and leaderboards.
  • To understand which parts of the game kids enjoy, so we can make it better.
  • We do NOT sell your data. We do NOT share it with advertisers. We do NOT show personalized ads to children.

👶COPPA — children's privacy

Fire Babys is directed at children under 13, so COPPA applies. That means:

  • Parental consent: our sign-in screen tells kids to ask a grown-up before entering an email.
  • Minimal collection: we only collect what's needed to run the game.
  • No behavioral advertising: kids never see ads based on tracking.
  • No open chat: multiplayer has no free-text chat — players join by lobby code only.
  • Parent rights: you can review, correct, or delete your child's personal information at any time.

🗑️Your rights — access & deletion

To see what data we hold for your child, correct it, or delete it entirely, email firebabysofficial@gmail.com with the subject "Data request". Tell us the email address used to sign in. We'll respond within 30 days. Deleting data means game progress and leaderboard entries are removed too.

🔐Security

We use industry-standard protections (encrypted connections, secure cloud storage). No system is perfect, but we work hard to keep your family's data safe — and we collect so little that there's very little to protect.

🌍Where data lives

Game data is stored on secure cloud servers (Cloudflare) in the United States. By playing, you agree to that storage.

📝Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in an important way, we'll post a notice on firebabys.io and update the date at the top. Big changes affecting kids' data will ask for fresh parental consent first.
