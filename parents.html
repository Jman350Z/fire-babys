🎓 A real fire-safety curriculum

We built Fire Babys with one purpose: teach children to prevent house fires — and what to do when a fire is nearby. These lessons appear as gameplay, story moments, and the in-game Fire Safety Academy:

  • 🚪 Get Outside & Stay Outside / Two Ways Out — know two ways out of every room; once you're out, never go back inside a burning building.
  • 🛑 Stop, Drop & Roll — if clothes catch fire: stop, drop to the ground, cover your face, and roll until the flames are out.
  • 🔌 Safe charging is a superpower — charge devices on hard, flat surfaces, never on pillows or beds.
  • 🔥 Know your fire classes (NFPA) — Class A (trash/wood) takes water; Class B (grease) must be smothered — never water; Class C (electrical) means cut the power — never water; Class D (metal) needs sand or dry powder — never water. The game teaches which tool beats which fire.

And the rule we repeat everywhere: real fire is never a toy — only grown-ups handle flame.

❓ FAQ

Is Fire Babys really free?

Yes — both games are completely free, with no purchases, no subscriptions, and no ads. Forever.

What ages is it for?

We designed it for roughly ages 6–12, but younger kids enjoy the characters and older kids enjoy the strategy. The reading level is simple, and dialogue can be tapped through at any pace.

Does it need internet?

Only to load the page the first time. After that, both games play offline. The worldwide leaderboard needs a connection to post scores, but the games are fully playable without it.

Can my child talk to strangers in the game?

No. There is no chat, no messaging, and no way for players to contact each other — in the games or on this site.

What data do you collect?

Practically none. Game progress is stored on your child's own device. The only thing ever sent anywhere is a display name and score, and only if they choose to post to the leaderboard.

My child's school wants fire-safety material. Can we use this?

We'd be honored. The in-game Fire Safety Academy covers NFPA fire classes, Stop Drop & Roll, and escape planning. Pair it with the free resources at sparkyschoolhouse.org for a full lesson.
