# FIRE BABYS SHOWS — Bible (set by Jeff, 2026-10-09)

**Format:** 31-second vertical (9:16) story clips for YouTube Shorts + TikTok. Three episodes a day.

**Style:** Angry Birds Toons-style silent slapstick clip comedy — NO birds. Big expressions, physical gags, fast cuts, 3–5 jokes per episode. Real ongoing story (the two teams rival, compete, and hang out), told in clips.

**The war:** Fire Babys vs Water Babys — games against each other, silly mini-wars, and dinners together. Rivals, never enemies: every episode ends on a friendship beat, everybody laughing.

**Language:** The characters NEVER say real words. They speak their own gibberish language only — Minions-style babble, squeaks, giggles, gasps, and funny burps. No dialogue, no captions with sentences (sound effects and reaction noises only).

**Cast:**
- Fire team: BLAZE (crowned leader), RUKO (red, black bandana, the scrapper), BOULDER (volcanic rock ball, the wall), SIZZLE (archer), CINDER (wild one, blue/purple flame tips), LORD INFERNO (armored boss).
- Water team: AQUA (crowned leader), SPLASH (water drop, black bandana), PEBBLE (ice-rock ball), DRIFT (archer), BUBBLE (wild splasher), LORD TIDAL (armored boss).

**Rules:** Kid-safe, LOL funny, slapstick only — nobody is ever really hurt, burnt out, or melted; everyone bounces back. Characters are gold: loved, never the butt of cruelty. Fire-safety heart where it fits naturally.
