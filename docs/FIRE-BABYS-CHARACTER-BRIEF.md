## THE TRANSFORMATION LAW (Jeff, 2026-10-20 — the most important rule)

- BASE FORM (unarmored, unsuited): the classic law in the GLOBAL RULES below — a
  spherical living fireball / waterball, the body IS the head, face on the ball.
  Unchanged. The GLOBAL RULES describe this base form unless they say otherwise.
- THE MORPH: the moment a Fire Baby or Water Baby puts on ANY suit, armor, mummy
  wraps, or gear, the character instantly MORPHS — the liquid fire or water
  surges, compresses, and expands into a sleek, athletic, fully articulated
  HUMANOID silhouette: distinct limbs, torso, agile posture, a high-end 3D
  action-figure build made of pure elemental energy. NOT human anatomy — an
  elemental energy mold, never flesh.
- ELEMENTAL RETENTION: the suited form keeps the character's living fire/water
  texture, roaring flame hair, glowing eyes, and facial expressions. The face
  and identity stay unmistakably THAT character.
- "BALL-IN-SUIT" IS BANNED: a static round ball stuffed inside a costume is a
  FAILED generation — reject it outright. Costumes do not fit over a ball;
  equipping gear triggers the morph. Showing the morph itself (the armory
  transformation sequence) is a signature beat — use it.
- SCOPE: this law governs SUITED scenes only. Unsuited scenes keep the classic
  ball law in full. The bans on beaks, noses, wings, feathers, and baked-in
  text apply in EVERY form, always.

## GLOBAL RULES — EVERY CHARACTER, EVERY FRAME

- A Fire Baby is a SPHERICAL living FIREBALL: a round flame-teardrop ball of toon-banded
  living fire (water characters: a round ball of living water). The BODY IS THE HEAD —
  one round ball, no separate torso, no human body, no human proportions. This is the
  BASE FORM — see THE TRANSFORMATION LAW above for what happens the moment gear goes on.
- Faces sit directly on the ball: big eyes with thick ANGRY-V eyebrows, red-hot pupils
  (fire team), and an expressive MOUTH (grin, smirk, gritted teeth). Faces are FLAME
  FACES — the mouth and brows are made of the character's own fire/water.
- NO beaks. NO noses. NO wings. NO feathers. NO birds of any kind — in ANY form.
  NO humans, NO people, NO caveman/cavegirl characters. In the BASE (unsuited) form:
  NO human bodies, NO legs in boots. (The SUITED form's elemental humanoid silhouette —
  limbs and torso of living fire/water — is the Transformation Law above; it is never
  human anatomy or flesh.)
- BASE FORM ONLY: stubby little arms with simple fists are fine (they grow straight
  out of the ball). Legs: none, or tiny stub feet at most — the ball is the body.
  (In the SUITED form, full articulated elemental limbs are required — see the
  Transformation Law.)
- Style: bold ink outlines, plushie-toyetic chunky shapes, vibrant Saturday-morning
  cartoon, squash-and-stretch slapstick. Characters are GOLD: loved, heroic, never
  harmed, never the butt of the joke.
- Costumes, suits, armor, and wraps TRIGGER THE TRANSFORMATION LAW: the moment gear
  goes on, the ball morphs into the sleek athletic humanoid elemental form (limbs,
  torso, elemental texture, flame hair, glowing eyes, face retained). A costume
  sitting on top of a static round ball — "ball-in-suit" — is a FAILED generation:
  reject it. (Supersedes the old "costumes sit on top of the ball" rule — Jeff,
  2026-10-20. Signature pieces that are part of a character's base design — crowns,
  bandanas, Boulder's rock plates — are identity, not gear; they are worn in base
  form exactly as the character blocks below describe.)
- Text ban: NO text, NO words, NO letters, NO numbers, NO signs, NO banners, NO logos,
  NO emblems, NO real-world brand patches anywhere in the image or video. Costumes are
  INSPIRED-BY and generic only — plain unmarked fabric and metal.

## FIRE TEAM

### BLAZE — the Star-Crowned (leader)
- Element/colors: FIRE. Golden-yellow living flame, orange flame edges.
- Silhouette/accessory: perfect round golden fireball wearing a GOLD CROWN with a big
  STAR on the front. Small star sparkles around him.
- Face: thick black angry-V brows, red-hot pupils, wide confident toothy grin.
- Personality: born leader, legendary smirk, main-character energy. "Follow me. Try to keep up."

### RUKO — the Hothead (brawler)
- Element/colors: FIRE. Deep red living flame, orange-yellow flame rim.
- Silhouette/accessory: compact round red fireball, BLACK BANDANA tied at the side,
  two chunky boxing fists raised.
- Face: thick black angry-V brows, red pupils, gritted-teeth snarl, sweat drops when straining.
- Personality: pure temper, fists ignite before his brain catches up, never walks away from a fight.

### BOULDER — the Gentle Wall (guardian)
- Element/colors: FIRE + ROCK. Orange flame face inside a ball of chunky dark-grey
  VOLCANIC ROCK PLATES with glowing lava seams.
- Silhouette/accessory: the biggest, roundest ball on the team — a rock-plated sphere;
  the plates ARE his body. One rocky fist is fine.
- Face: heavy black angry-V brows, red pupils, small flat unimpressed mouth.
- Personality: soft-spoken, immovable, loyal heart — "Nobody touches my friends. NOBODY."

### SIZZLE — the Showoff (archer)
- Element/colors: FIRE. Bright yellow-orange living flame.
- Silhouette/accessory: round yellow fireball, BLACK BANDANA WITH RED CHEVRON pattern,
  holds a small wooden BOW (her trick-shot bow).
- Face: sharp angry-V brows, red pupils, cocky side smirk.
- Personality: lean, fast, allergic to humility — every hit is a championship celebration.

### CINDER — the Chaos Gremlin (pyro)
- Element/colors: FIRE. Orange-yellow flame body with BLUE AND PURPLE flame tips on top.
- Silhouette/accessory: round fireball with a wild multi-color flame crest, stubby arms raised.
- Face: wild angled brows, huge manic grin full of pointy teeth, tongue out.
- Personality: unpredictable, giggles while things detonate. Harmless chaos, never cruel.

### LORD INFERNO — the Wildfire King (boss)
- Element/colors: FIRE. Enormous deep-red flame.
- Silhouette/accessory: giant round red flame head wearing a BLACK SPIKED HORNED HELMET
  and dark spiked armor pieces around the ball; jagged rock-tooth grin guard at the chin.
  (In Jeff's sheets he also appears as a full armored figure — for video, keep him a giant
  round flame ball in the horned helmet and armor so he matches the team.)
- Face: heavy angry-V brows, red pupils, gritted rock teeth.
- Personality: ancient, arrogant, enormous — the boss, played big and theatrical, still kid-safe.

## THE SKELETONS — "the Bonehead crew" (comic villains)
- Goofy cartoon skeletons: big round white skulls, silly grins, expressive worried or
  dizzy faces. Sneaky but harmless. They get bonked, toppled and spun; they crumble into
  harmless bone piles and always shake it off giggling. Kid-safe: no gore, no horror,
  no glowing evil eyes. They are the joke's target — the Fire Babys never are.

## CANON — MOON BASE INVASION & MUTUAL DEFENSE (File 2, Jeff 2026-10-20)

The core epic narrative for cinematic trailers and viral shorts:
1. THE ALERT: the Water Babys (Aqua, Splash, Pebble, Drift, Bubble, Lord Tidal) are
   on patrol at the Water Moon Base when Underworld Slime Cruisers and Skeleton
   Starfighters ambush the base with sticky oil-bombs. The Water Babys transmit an
   emergency quantum signal to Earth.
2. THE ARMORY TRANSFORMATION: the Fire Babys receive the signal and step into
   advanced titanium cosmic space-suits in the armory. As the heavy seals clamp
   shut, the Transformation Law fires: the spherical fireballs expand and snap
   into athletic, heroic humanoid fire-warriors in glowing armored spacesuits.
3. ZERO-G COUNTER-ATTACK & MUTUAL RESCUE: the Fire Babys warp into orbit alongside
   the Water Babys. Combat is fast, comedic, and high-octane. Heroism is MUTUAL:
   when Ruko (in his space-suit) is trapped in a slime web, Aqua and Bubble dive
   in, shatter the locks, and carry him to safety — the Water Babys protect the
   Fire Babys just as fiercely as the Fire Babys protect them.

gets a 3D cinematic short reenacting its actual gameplay mechanics in exaggerated,
premium cartoon action. Suited games use the Transformation Law (e.g., Slingshot

activate tectonic armor and become towering stone-plated juggernauts over humanoid
energy cores). 15–30 seconds per video, single clear gag, rich 3D cinematic depth.
