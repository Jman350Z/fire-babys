🎓 FIRE SAFETY ACADEMY

Real lessons hiding inside the game.
  • 🔥 Know your fire classes — A (trash), B (grease), C (electrical), D (metal). Every fire needs the right tool — and water is NOT always the answer!
  • 🛑 Stop, Drop & Roll — if your clothes catch fire, smother the flames and roll until they're out.
  • 🚪 Get Out & Stay Out — two ways out of every room. Once you're out, never go back in.
  • 🔌 Charge safe — devices charge on hard, flat surfaces. Pillows and beds are a no-go.
