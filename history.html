👶 Meet the Fire Babys

Fire Babys are gold — precious, loved, and never the butt of the joke. Each one is a living flame with a big personality:

🔥 Blaze

The leader. A round, glowing orange-yellow fire baby with a happy expression and a brave heart. Always first into adventure, always looking out for friends.

😡 Ruko

The hothead. A bright red fire baby with a black combat headband and a frenzied expression. Quick to act, fierce in loyalty, learning that staying calm saves the day.

✨ Sizzle

The spark. Quick, clever, and always crackling with ideas. Sizzle can solve any puzzle — just don't ask them to sit still.

🪨 Boulder

The strong one. Big, steady, and unmovable. Boulder protects the smaller Fire Babys and never backs down from a challenge.

💎 Cinder

The gentle soul. Soft-spoken and kind, with a heart as warm as their glow. Cinder reminds everyone that fire can comfort as well as dazzle.

👑 Lord Inferno

The ancient one. A wise and powerful elder flame who has watched over the kingdoms for a thousand years. Stern but fair — and secretly fond of the young ones' antics.

🎮 The Games Begin

The First Spark

Jeffery Pruden discovers the Fire Babys and knows the world needs to meet them. The mission is set: teach children fire safety through play.

SPLASH Takes Over

The flagship game launches — grab, carry, DUNK! Two teams, one pool, endless laughs. SPLASH becomes the only promoted mode.

Fire World Opens

55 games. One page. Every game teaches a fire-safety lesson. Built by Jman350Z & the fire team, free for every child on Earth.

The $10,000 Competition

Creators around the world build their own Fire Babys game modes. One winner ships in the official phone app and takes home $10,000.

Today

The Fire Babys are on a mission to become the #1 fire safety awareness brand in the world — and to make every child fall in love with them along the way.

❤️ Our Promise

Fire Babys are gold. We treat them with love. They are never shown harmed, destroyed, or as the joke. They teach children to prevent house fires and what to do when a fire is nearby — through laughter, adventure, and play.

Every game is 100% kid-safe. No chat with strangers. No purchases. No tricks. Just fun that could save a life.
