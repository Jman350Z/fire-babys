🔥What is Fire Babys?

Fire Babys is a free kid-safe battle game with one big purpose: teach children to prevent house fires — and what to do when a fire is nearby. You build a crew of living fireball heroes, battle fire-monsters, and pick up real fire-safety lessons (like which tool beats which class of fire) along the way.

💰Is it free?

Yes — 100% free to play. No downloads cost money, no in-game purchases right now. If that ever changes, parents will hear about it first.

🛡️Is it safe for my kid?

Yes. Fire Babys is built kid-safe from the ground up:

  • COPPA compliant — we follow U.S. children's privacy law. See our Privacy Policy.
  • No open chat — multiplayer has no free-text chat. Players join private lobbies using a code.
  • No ads, no data selling — ever.
  • Parent gate — the sign-in screen tells kids to ask a grown-up first.

🌐How does multiplayer work?

Hit MULTIPLAYER in the 3D game, then create a lobby or join with a code a friend shares. Lobbies hold up to 30 players. Pick Co-op (everyone vs. the fire together) or Versus (2v2, 4v4, 6v6, 7v7, or 15v15 team battles with attack, block, and double-jump combat). Only people with your lobby code can join.

🎮What are the game modes?

  • Story — cinematic adventure across the Ember towns.
  • Free Roam — explore Ember Valley at your own pace.
  • Practice — learn the ropes, no pressure.
  • Tournament — climb the bracket.
  • Rescue — save the day against the clock.
  • Survival — how long can you last?
  • Multiplayer — co-op & versus with up to 30 players.

📧How do I sign in?

When you first visit firebabys.io, you'll be asked for an email address (kids: ask a grown-up first!). That email becomes your account — your progress and settings stay on your device. One sign-in works across every page of the site.

🗑️How do I delete my / my child's data?

Email firebabysofficial@gmail.com with the subject "Data request" and the sign-in email address. We'll delete everything within 30 days — progress and leaderboard entries included.

📱Does it work on phones?

Yes! Both games are built mobile-first and run right in your phone's browser — no app download needed. (Native iOS/Android apps are on the roadmap.)

🎓What fire-safety lessons are in the game?

Real ones: fire classes A/B/C/D and the right tool for each, Stop-Drop-and-Roll, Get Out and Stay Out, two ways out of every room, and safe device charging. The in-game Fire Safety Academy collects them all.
