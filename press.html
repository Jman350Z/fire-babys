FIRE BABYS
The fire-safety battle game for kids

✏️The one-paragraph version

Fire Babys is a free, kid-safe battle game with a mission: teach children to prevent house fires — and what to do when a fire is nearby. Players build a crew of living fireball heroes, battle fire-monsters across a 2D battler and a 3D open world, and absorb real fire-safety lessons (fire classes, Stop-Drop-and-Roll, escape planning) through play. Online multiplayer supports up to 30 players in co-op and team-versus modes.

📖The longer story

Most kids' games are pure entertainment. Fire Babys starts from a different question: what if the game your kid can't put down also taught them how to survive a house fire? Every system serves that purpose — enemies carry real fire classes (A/B/C/D) so kids learn the right tool for each fire, the in-game Fire Safety Academy collects NFPA-aligned lessons, and the whole experience is wrapped in a glossy, toyetic world kids actually want to explore. It's COPPA-compliant, has no ads and no open chat, and it's completely free.

🎯What makes it different

  • Education baked in, not bolted on — fire classes, extinguish matchups, and escape planning are core mechanics, not pop quizzes.
  • Real multiplayer for kids, done safely — up to 30 players, lobby codes only, no open chat.
  • Toyetic by design — characters are round, bouncy living embers. Zero beaks, wings, or feathers, ever.
  • Plays anywhere — runs in any modern phone or desktop browser. No download.
