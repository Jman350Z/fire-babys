1. My fellow nerds — I need your help 🔥

October 5, 2026 — Jeff

I'm Jeff, and I'm building a kids' battle game called Fire Babys. Right now it's a concept with a big heart: a fun, chaotic, Fall Guys-style game where little fire babies battle it out. Our flagship mode is called SPLASH — two teams, two pools, grab your rivals and dunk them. Last team standing wins.

But here's the twist: it's secretly a fire-awareness teacher. Fire classes, extinguishers, what to do when a fire is nearby — baked right into the gameplay. Our goal is for game proceeds to help start and fund real US fire-department school-awareness programs.
