MISSION

My fellow nerds — I need your help 🔥

October 5, 2026 — Jeff

I'm Jeff, and I'm building a kids' battle game called Fire Babys. Right now it's a concept with a big heart: a fun, chaotic, Fall Guys-style game where little fire babies battle it out. Our flagship mode is called SPLASH — two teams, two pools, grab your rivals and dunk them. Last team standing wins.

But here's the twist: it's secretly a fire-awareness teacher. Fire classes, extinguishers, what to do when a fire is nearby — baked right into the gameplay. And every dollar this game makes is going to REAL fire awareness programs with actual US fire departments, going into schools and teaching kids face to face.

I'm looking for:
🎮 Playtesters — play the early concept and tell me what's fun and what's broken
🧠 Nerds with opinions — devs, artists, game-design brains, I want your takes
📣 Believers — if this mission hits you, spread the word

This is day one. Come build it with me.
