🎓What your child learns

Fire Babys isn't just fun — it's a fire-safety curriculum disguised as a game. Here's what's actually being taught:

🔥
Fire classes A / B / C / D. Not every fire is the same — and water doesn't fix all of them. Kids learn which tool beats which class through the extinguish matchup system (correct counter = bonus damage; wrong tool can backfire!).
🛑
Stop, Drop & Roll. If clothes catch fire: stop moving, drop to the ground, cover your face, and roll until flames are out.
🚪
Get Out & Stay Out. Two ways out of every room. Once you're outside, never go back in — not for toys, not for pets. Firefighters do the rescuing.
🗺️
Family escape plans. The game nudges kids to talk with you about your home's plan: where's the meeting spot outside?
🔌
Everyday prevention. Safe device charging, keeping flammables from heat, and why only grown-ups handle flame.
⚠️ Please reinforce at home: the game is a starting point, not a substitute for real practice. Walk your escape plan together — it takes 10 minutes and it matters.

⏱️Screen-time tips

  • Play together first. The first 15 minutes with your child tells you everything about what they're experiencing.
  • Use natural breakpoints. Story chapters and Survival runs end cleanly — great stopping points.
  • Talk about the lessons. "Which tool beat that grease fire?" turns game time into learning time.
  • Bedtime buffer. Like any exciting game, give 30 minutes of wind-down before sleep.

🌐How multiplayer keeps kids safe

  • No open chat. There is no free-text chat anywhere in the game — nothing to moderate, nothing for strangers to abuse.
  • Lobby codes only. Your child can only play with people who share the same lobby code — typically friends and family.
  • No friend lists, no DMs. Multiplayer is session-based; nobody can follow or message your child.
  • Display names, not real names. We encourage fun nicknames — never use a real name, school, or address.
  • You can always watch. Multiplayer happens on one shared screen per device — sit alongside and see everything.
