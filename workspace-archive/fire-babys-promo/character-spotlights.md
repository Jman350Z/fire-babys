# Fire Babys Character Spotlights — "Meet the Team" series
6 posts, one per character. Pair each with character art. Canonical titles from Jeff's lineup poster (2026-10-03).

---

## 1. BLAZE ⭐ • the Star-Crowned
The hero — that's you. Golden star crown, confident smirk, no counter specialty yet and no weaknesses either: just heart, hustle, and a Fire Nova that hits everything. Every legend starts somewhere.
**Quirk:** Practices his hero pose in every reflective surface. The volcano's lava pool. Puddles. Boulder's shiny head.

## 2. RUKO 🔥 • the Hothead
Team leader with a black skull headband and big boxing fists. Charges in fists first, asks questions later. His Fist Flurry melts Class-A fires in seconds... but put him near a grease fire and you'll get a flare-up and a very embarrassed leader.
**Quirk:** Celebrates every win with a victory spin that showers sparks everywhere. The team has learned to duck.

## 3. BOULDER 🪨 • the Gentle Wall
Slow, round, covered in rock plates, immovable. Boulder doesn't dodge — he stands in front of you and dares the fire to try. His Magma Taunt pulls every flame toward him, because he'd rather burn than let a friend get singed.
**Quirk:** Falls asleep standing up. Has slept through two entire battles. Woke up, asked "did we win?", went back to sleep.

## 4. SIZZLE 🏹 • the Showoff
Never misses — and she'll tell you about it. Her Spark Volley picks off flames one precise arrow at a time. The one you call when the fire is somewhere delicate. Cool under pressure, showoff the rest of the time.
**Quirk:** Polishes her arrows when she's nervous. Before the Inferno fight she polished them for an hour. Nobody had the heart to stop her.

## 5. CINDER ☄️ • the Chaos Gremlin
Wild blue-purple flames, tongue out, zero impulse control. Fights fire WITH fire and it's exactly as chaotic as it sounds. Her Meteor Drop is the biggest boom on the team — spectacular against grease fires, absolutely banned near anything wooden. Ruko has a whistle. It's for Cinder.
**Quirk:** Leaves tiny fire footprints when she's excited. The team follows them when she wanders off, which is often.

## 6. LORD INFERNO 🌋 • the Wildfire King
Horned helmet, spiked armor, and an ego the size of his volcano. He doesn't want to rule the Seven Kingdoms — he wants to BURN them. His Cataclysm Slam is the reason the Underworld Kingdom stays hidden: nobody who's gone through the volcano has come back to talk about it.
**Quirk:** Monologues. Long ones. The heroes once beat him mid-speech and he asked them to "at least let me finish the dramatic part."
