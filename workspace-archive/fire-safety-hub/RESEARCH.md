## Findings that shape the hub

- **Link-first is USFA's own model.** USFA builds these pages as shareable outreach: pictograph pages even ship a ready-made animation with an invitation to share it on your website and social channels along with their suggested message, and the image galleries state the photos are free for outreach/training use. The hub therefore *links* (and could later embed 1–2 pictograph animations with attribution) instead of mirroring content that would go stale.
- **Fire Prevention Week note:** FPW (each October) is led nationally by the NFPA; USFA runs no separate FPW landing page (search-verified 2026-10-21). USFA's home for the season is Fire Is Everyone's Fight (#10), whose calendar carries the October slot — so the hub's Fire Prevention Week card links to FIEF. If Jeff later wants the NFPA FPW page linked too, that's a one-line addition.
- **Dead end found:** the old USFA "Digital Media Library" address (`/prevention/outreach/media/`, still cited in USFA's own older FIEF outreach guide) returns **404** as of 2026-10-21. Its living equivalents are the per-topic image galleries and pictograph library verified above — the hub links those instead.
- **Positioning:** the hub says "for everyone" throughout and never claims to be "for kids," matching the standing positioning law. Teacher/family sections are framed by audience (educators, families), not by child-audience claims.
