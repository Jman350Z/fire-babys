Start Here — the house rules

  • Real fire is never a toy. Always get a grown-up.
  • If there is a fire: get out, stay out, and call 911 from outside.
  • Get low under smoke and go to your outside meeting place.
  • If your clothes ever catch fire: stop, drop, and roll.

Everything below is the deeper version of these rules, written by the U.S. Fire Administration and linked — never copied — so you always get the current official guidance.

🍳 Cooking — and the Slimer lesson

Cooking starts more home fires than anything else. Stay with your pan, keep a lid close, and keep anything that can burn away from the stove.

The Slimer lesson: “Oil fire? Never spray it — smother it, and get a grown-up.” Water makes a grease fire spread. A lid slid over the pan takes away its air instead.

For: anyone who cooks

🚪 Escape Planning

Draw a map of your home, mark two ways out of every room, pick an outside meeting place — then practice it like a drill until everyone can do it fast, even in the dark.

🌡️ Heating

Space heaters, fireplaces, and wood stoves all need room to breathe: keep anything that can burn at least three feet away, plug portable heaters straight into the wall, and never heat a home with a stove.

🎓 The Fire Babys Fire-Safety Academy

These are the lessons the Fire Babys games teach — collected here from firebabys.io so the teaching never weighs the games down:

  • 🚪 Get Outside & Stay Outside / Two Ways Out — know two ways out of every room; once you're out, never go back inside a burning building.
  • 🛑 Stop, Drop & Roll — if clothes catch fire: stop, drop to the ground, cover your face, and roll until the flames are out.
  • 🔌 Safe charging is a superpower — charge devices on hard, flat surfaces, never on pillows or beds.
  • 🔥 Know your fire classes (NFPA) — Class A (trash/wood) takes water; Class B (grease) must be smothered — never water; Class C (electrical) means cut the power — never water; Class D (metal) needs sand or dry powder — never water. The game teaches which tool beats which fire.

And the rule we repeat everywhere: real fire is never a toy — only grown-ups handle flame.

🔥 Fire-safety tips from the games

Every Fire Babys game card used to carry one of these one-line lessons. They all live here now:

  • A slingshot is a toy — never aim at people, pets, or anything that can break. Real fire is not a toy either.
  • In a real emergency, stay calm and move quickly to safety — panic makes things worse!
  • Never jump over real fire — get LOW, get OUT, and call 911!
  • Ice and water cool things down — but NEVER touch ice on a real frozen lake without a grown-up!
  • Boulders are dangerous — in real life, NEVER climb on rocky cliffs or near volcanoes!
  • Cool water puts out heat — but if YOU get hurt, tell a grown-up right away!
  • Rhythm keeps you calm — in an emergency, slow breaths help you think clearly!
  • Always look where you're going — climbing high places needs a grown-up nearby!
  • Defending your home starts with prevention — check smoke alarms every month!
  • Stacked things can topple — keep heavy items low and away from heat!
  • Lava is VERY hot — in real life, stay far away from anything burning or melting!
  • Pinball is all fun — but real machines run on electricity. Never poke inside them!
  • Fast feet save lives — practice moving quickly AND safely to your family's meeting spot!
  • Hold on tight in life too — always use handrails on stairs and playgrounds!
  • Think before you act — having a plan keeps everyone safe, just like a fire escape plan!
  • Heavy things fall fast — never stand under anything that could drop on you!
  • Running from danger is smart — but always run TOWARD a grown-up or an exit, never deeper inside!
  • Pushing and shoving is never safe play — in games AND in real life, keep your hands to yourself!
  • Lava is pretend-hot, but real stoves are REALLY hot — never touch without a grown-up!
  • Climbing is fun on playgrounds — but never climb on stoves, heaters, or anything hot!
  • Water puts out small fires! But firefighters handle big ones — always get help from a grown-up!
  • Match the right tool to the job — water for small fires, but ALWAYS call 911 for big ones!
  • Catch falling stars in games — but NEVER try to catch anything falling in real life! Let it drop and step back!
  • In smoky air, stay LOW where the air is clearer — smoke rises, so crawl under it!
  • Plan your escape route BEFORE there's an emergency — draw your family's fire escape map today!
  • Paddles are for ping-pong, not for hitting! Play safe and take turns!
  • Think before you act! A good plan keeps everyone safe — in games and in fire drills!
  • Hot things melt ice and burn skin! Never touch stoves, ovens, or hot pans.
  • Climb UP to safety! In a fire, never use elevators — take the stairs.
  • Smoke rises! If there's smoke, stay LOW where the air is cleaner.
  • Defend your home! Make sure smoke alarms work — test them monthly.
  • Wind spreads fire fast! On windy days, be extra careful around anything hot.
  • Aim water at the BASE of a fire! That's where the flames feed.
  • Sparks fly! Keep a safe distance from campfires and fireworks.
  • A moat of water stops fire! In real life, close doors to slow fire spread.
  • Molten rock is 2000°F! Real volcanoes are beautiful but stay far away.
  • Volcanoes are dangerous! If one erupts near you, follow evacuation orders immediately.
  • Ropes save lives! Firefighters use ropes and ladders — always have an escape route.
  • Think ahead! Plan TWO ways out of every room before you need them.
  • Never throw things at others — aim only at safe targets, and never play with real fire!
  • Lava is always pretend in games — real hot liquids cause serious burns. Never touch!
  • Playful bumping is for games only — never push or shove real people!
  • Timing matters! When the smoke alarm beeps, act FAST — get out right away!
  • Slippery floors are dangerous — walk, don't run, on wet surfaces!
  • Firefighters are the heroes who battle real blazes — always let grown-ups handle fire!
  • Sorting and organizing keeps things safe — keep matches sorted away from kids!
  • Smoke is dangerous — if you see smoke, get LOW under it and get OUT!
  • Teamwork saves lives! In an emergency, help each other get to safety!
  • Running fast is fun in games — but in a real fire, WALK quickly and never push!
  • Ropes and swings are playground fun — never swing near fires or hot stoves!
  • Water cools fire! But only grown-ups and firefighters should battle real flames!
  • Throwing is for safe targets only — never aim at people or animals!
  • Falling is scary — always use handrails on stairs and never play near cliffs!
  • Bumping is for games — in real life, keep your hands to yourself!
  • Legends stay safe! Know your escape plan, test alarms monthly, and NEVER play with fire!
  • If your clothes catch fire — STOP, DROP, and ROLL!
  • If you see fire, tell a grown-up right away — never try to fight it yourself!
  • Real fire is never a toy — in a real emergency, get out and get a grown-up!
