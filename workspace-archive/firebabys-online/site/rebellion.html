The Invasion of Fire World

Why We Fight

The homeland is Fire World — mountains of living ember, rivers of bright water, and at its heart the great Caldera, where the first Fire Babys were born and where the Water Babys of the deep lakes have always been family. It is home. It is worth every risk.

The Underworld struck from below. Jealous of the light on the surface, it tore open the tunnels under the Caldera and poured its army up through the cracks. First came the Slimers — blobs of living oil that ooze through streets and swallow everything bright. Then the Bonehead crew marched in behind them, locking captured homelanders in their own storerooms and raising the Underworld's banner over the Caldera.

The Rebellion answers quietly. An army can't retake streets that the Slimers have slicked — but one hero can slip through them. Lord Inferno and Lord Tidal hand-picked twelve heroes of fire and water. Your job: infiltrate the occupied districts, free the captured, shut down the Slime Gates feeding the invasion, and get out unseen — mission by mission, street by street, until Fire World is free again.

Field Manual

How It Plays

Rebellion is a stealth action-adventure. Brains first, bravery always — here's the craft every hero trains in.

1

Stay Unseen

Every Bonehead patrol sweeps a cone of vision ahead of it, and Slimers sense heat and ripples. Watch the cones. Move when backs turn. Shadows, rooftops, and flood channels are your roads — the open street belongs to the enemy.

2

Gear Up — Transform

At home, a hero is a round ball of living fire or water. The moment a hero kits up in tactical gear, the transformation takes hold: the ball unfolds into a sleek, athletic humanoid of living element — flame or water hair streaming, eyes glowing, ready for anything. Gear isn't a costume. It's the call to duty made visible.

3

Rescue the Homelanders

Captured Fire Babys and Water Babys are locked up across every occupied district. Pick the locks, calm the nerves, and guide your people to extraction. A mission only counts when everybody gets home.

4

Sabotage the Slime Gates

The Slime Gates are the invasion's supply line — tear them down and the Slimers stop coming. And when a Slimer blocks your path, remember the first rule of the Rebellion: smother it with ash and earth. Never spray it with water — water only spreads the oil.
