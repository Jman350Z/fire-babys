Fire Safety — Read This

DON’T PLAY WITH FIRE.

Fire Babys is a pretend fantasy game. The Babys are fictional characters. Nothing you see in this game should ever be tried in real life.

  • Real fire is never safe. It is never a toy and never a game.
  • Never play with matches, lighters, candles, stoves, grills, or fireworks.
  • Kids should never handle fire. Fire is for grown-ups only.
  • Playing with real fire can burn you, burn your house down, and kill.

If anyone is hurt or any property is damaged because of real fire, that is NOT the fault or responsibility of Jeff Pruden or Fire Babys.

In a fire emergency, get to safety and call your local emergency number immediately.
