# FIRE BABYS SHOWS — Episode Bank Ep02–Ep10
Prepared 2026-10-09 (for Sat Oct 10 – Mon Oct 12, 3/day). Each: 31s, vertical 9:16, silent slapstick, gibberish-only voices (babble/squeaks/burps, NO real words), 3–5 gags, ends on friendship beat. Characters are gold — nobody hurt, burnt out, or melted; everyone bounces back laughing.

Cast shorthand — Fire: Blaze (leader), Ruko (scrapper), Boulder (wall), Sizzle (archer), Cinder (wild), Lord Inferno (boss). Water: Aqua (leader), Splash, Pebble, Drift (archer), Bubble (wild), Lord Tidal (boss).

## Ep02 — "Slingshot Snack War" (game/war)
0–4s: Teams face off across a picnic blanket, melon-catapult each side. 4–10s: Sizzle launches a grape — Drift's water-arrow pops it mid-air into juice rain. 10–17s: Cinder over-pumps the catapult, launches HIMSELF, lands in a cream pie, tongue out. 17–24s: Bubble returns fire with a giant bubble that carries Boulder slowly away; Boulder waves politely. 24–31s: Pie + juice everywhere, both teams slip-slide into one pile, laughing, sharing the last melon slice together.

## Ep03 — "Dinner Disaster Date" (dinner together)
0–5s: One long table, Fire side cooking, Water side chilling drinks. 5–11s: Ruko's chili bubbles over — Splash "helps" with a water jet, makes a rainbow steam cloud. 11–18s: Cinder burps a tiny blue flame, lights the birthday-style candles on a cake; Aqua applauds. 18–25s: Boulder tries to sit, chair shrinks him into a tiny stool — Pebble builds him an ice throne. 25–31s: Everyone toasts with marshmallow sticks, group giggle, burp chorus (musical, 3 notes).

## Ep04 — "Soccer Splash Cup" (game)
0–4s: Pebble is the ball. He loves it. 4–10s: Blaze kicks — Pebble rolls through a puddle, grows a water afro. 10–16s: Sizzle's trick shot bends around Lord Tidal, who spins like a top. 16–23s: Bubble scores an own-goal backwards, celebrates anyway, confetti water. 23–31s: Final kick: everyone kicks at once, Pebble rockets up, parachutes down into a group hug. Trophy = a shared ice-cream.

## Ep05 — "The Great Blanket Fort War" (war → dinner)
0–5s: Two blanket forts, flags up (flame flag / wave flag). 5–12s: Pillow catapults; Ruko's pillow boomerangs back onto his own head. 12–18s: Aqua floods the moat (a kiddie pool) — Cinder surfs it on a dinner tray. 18–25s: Forts merge by accident into one mega-fort. 25–31s: Both teams inside, flashlight giggles, snack sharing. War over: best fort ever.

## Ep06 — "Cook-Off Chaos" (dinner/competition)
0–5s: Blaze vs Aqua cook-off, Lord Inferno & Lord Tidal as giant-hat judges. 5–11s: Sizzle juggles tomatoes; Drift juggles ice cubes; they swap mid-air — salad snowstorm. 11–18s: Cinder tastes soup, hair-flames turn rainbow, happy spin. 18–24s: Judges taste both, hats pop off, both win. 24–31s: Everyone eats both dishes at one table, burp-applause.

## Ep07 — "Hide & Seek Volcano" (game, Fire Babys homeland)
0–5s: Counting (gibberish numbers = funny honks). Boulder "hides" behind a tiny plant. 5–12s: Splash hides IN a giant bubble, floats away, waves. 12–18s: Ruko finds Cinder by his giggling flame-tips behind a rock. 18–25s: Pebble still unfound — he's been the "rock" next to them all along. 25–31s: Everyone laughs, Pebble gets the winner's crown of flowers.

## Ep08 — "Dance Battle Burp-Off" (game)
0–5s: Dance floor: lava tiles vs wave tiles. 5–11s: Bubble's breakdance splashes the crowd (happy). 11–17s: Boulder does one single stomp — whole floor bounces, everyone airborne, slow-mo faces. 17–24s: Burp-beatbox duet: Cinder vs Splash, 4 beats, crowd claps. 24–31s: Both teams conga line off together, Lord Inferno & Lord Tidal doing tiny awkward robot.

## Ep09 — "The Runaway Picnic Basket" (story chase)
0–5s: Basket rolls downhill; both teams chase. 5–12s: Drift shoots a water-net, catches a beach ball instead. 12–18s: Sizzle's lasso-arrow ties the basket to Cinder — Cinder water-skis on grass behind it. 18–25s: Basket stops at a cliff of flowers, pops open — it's full of baby sandwiches shaped like the characters. 25–31s: Picnic on the hill, sunset, everyone eating mini-themselves, giggling.

## Ep10 — "Goodnight Truce" (dinner/bedtime closer)
0–5s: Campfire (safe ring, Fire Babys tending it properly — fire-safety heart: stones ring, water bucket beside it, Aqua in charge of the bucket). 5–12s: Marshmallow swaps: Fire toasts, Water cools with a tiny mist. 12–19s: Ghost story in gibberish — Lord Tidal scares himself, hides behind Pebble. 19–26s: Fireflies/stars, Cinder yawns a tiny heart-shaped puff. 26–31s: Both teams asleep in one pile, "gibberish snore" chorus, Blaze pulls a blanket over everyone.

## Production notes
- Voices: one gibberish pass per character (pitch-mapped: Boulder/Tidal low, Cinder/Bubble high), burps as punctuation only, never gross-out.
- No real words anywhere: no dialogue, no sentence captions; on-screen text limited to names/stars/hearts.
- End-card (last 1s, every ep): flame+wave heart logo, no text except "FIRE BABYS".
- Fire-safety heart where natural (Ep10 campfire ring + water bucket) — never a lecture.
