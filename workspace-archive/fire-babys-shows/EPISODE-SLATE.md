# Fire Babys Shows — Episode Slate (31s each, ~3 gags, ends on friendship)

Format reminder: vertical, gibberish only, slapstick, nobody ever really hurt. Ruko = fire scrapper, Splash = water rival, Boulder/Pebble = the walls, Sizzle/Drift = archers, Cinder/Bubble = wild ones, Blaze/Aqua = leaders, Lord Inferno/Lord Tidal = boss cameos.

- **Ep 1 — Melon Mayhem** ✅ BUILT (2026-10-09): melon soccer; Boulder wobble-block, Splash headbutt bonks Drift, Cinder rockets into a bush.
- **Ep 2 — Tug of War**: rope over a mud puddle; Boulder anchors, Pebble anchors; Bubble slips, whole line pile-up in the mud; both teams crack up, mud mustaches.
- **Ep 3 — Dinner Disaster**: one big soup pot, both teams cooking; Cinder sneezes a spark in, pot volcanoes; Splash surfs the soup wave to safety; everyone slurps, giant group burp.
- **Ep 4 — Race Day**: Blaze vs Aqua flag start; Boulder and Pebble roll downhill like bowling balls; tie at the finish, dizzy stars, shared trophy wobble.
- **Ep 5 — The Kite War**: Sizzle and Drift fly rival kites; strings tangle, the two spin together like a top, land giggling in a haystack.
- **Ep 6 — Birthday Boom**: Blaze's cake, 100 candles; every blow makes them flare bigger; Splash's party horn douses them all at once; confetti rain, cake for everyone.
