## Full Description

🔥 WELCOME TO FIRE WORLD 🔥

One world, 50+ free games, and the whole Fire Babys squad — Blaze, Aqua, Ruko, Boulder, Sizzle, Cinder and more — ready to play!

Fire World is the home of the Fire Babys: a big, friendly game world packed with runners, racers, puzzles, defenders, arcade challenges and more. Every game is free, quick to pick up, and built for short, fun sessions — at home, on a break, anywhere.

**INSIDE FIRE WORLD:**
🎮 50+ games in one hub — Blaze Runner, Slingshot Cascade, Inferno Drop, Pyra Prime Defense and dozens more
👥 Real multiplayer — jump into lobbies and play with friends (co-op and versus)
🏆 Leaderboards — post your scores and climb the board
🧑‍🚀 Your own player profile — create an account, build your Fire Baby, and link your accounts
🌍 117 languages — play in your language, picked right inside the game
🎃 Seasons & events — new themes roll through the world all year, starting with the Halloween Candy Moon Caper

**THE HEART OF IT:**
Fire Babys exists to teach fire safety through play — real tips woven into the fun, like why you smother an oil fire instead of spraying it. The characters are the stars, the safety is real, and the games are for everyone.

**FREE — REALLY:**
No ads. No in-app purchases. No chat. Just games. The same Fire World also plays free in your browser at firebabys.io, where a full offline download is available too.

the future is BLAZING FOR FIRE BABYS 🔥
