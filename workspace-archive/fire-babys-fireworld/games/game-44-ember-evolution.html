🔥 Ember Evolution 🔥

Slide the cute embers to merge them and evolve them up the flame ladder!

✨ Spark → Flicker → Flame → Blaze → Inferno → 🔥 PHOENIX 🔥

How to play: Swipe (or use arrow keys / WASD) to slide all tiles. When two matching embers touch, they merge into the next, bigger, happier stage — with a pop! The game ends when the grid is full and no moves are left. Can you reach the PHOENIX?

🔥 Fire Safety Lesson: Small embers can become big fires! Always fully put out campfires — drown it, stir it, and feel for heat.
