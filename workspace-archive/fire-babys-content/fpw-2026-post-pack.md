## POST 1 — The theme (pairs with an art image)

🔥 It's FIRE PREVENTION WEEK (Oct 4–10) and this year's mission is one every family needs to hear: SAFE CHARGING IS A SUPERPOWER 🔌⚡

Your phone, laptop, toys, e-bike — they all run on lithium-ion batteries, and the wrong charger or charging on the couch can start a real fire.

Charge like a hero:
✅ Use the charger that came with your device
✅ Charge on a hard surface — never the bed or couch
✅ Unplug when it's done — don't overcharge

The Fire Babys train every day so real families stay safe. Start today.
