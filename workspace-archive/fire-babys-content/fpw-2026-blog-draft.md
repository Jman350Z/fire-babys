<h2>2. Fire Prevention Week 2026: Safe Charging Is a Superpower 🔌🔥</h2>
<div class="date">October 5, 2026 — Fire Babys</div>
<p>It's Fire Prevention Week (October 4–10) — the one week a year the whole country talks about stopping fires before they start. This year's national theme from the NFPA is <b>"Charge into Fire Safety: Safe Charging Is a Superpower"</b> — and it's about something sitting in almost every room of your house right now: lithium-ion batteries.</p>
<p>Phones, laptops, tablets, toys, power tools, e-bikes, scooters — they all store a huge amount of energy in a tiny space. Charged wrong, they can overheat, catch fire, or even explode. The fix is simple, and it's a perfect thing to teach your kids this week:</p>
<p>✅ <b>Use the cord and charger that came with the device</b> — or one the manufacturer approves. Cheap knockoff chargers are how batteries get hurt.<br>✅ <b>Charge on a hard surface.</b> Never on the bed, couch, or under a pillow — heat needs somewhere to go.<br>✅ <b>Unplug when it's done.</b> Don't overcharge, and don't charge overnight where nobody's watching.<br>✅ <b>Recycle old batteries properly.</b> Never in the trash or recycling bin — take them to a battery drop-off.</p>
<p>And while you're in fire-safety mode: <b>test your smoke alarms</b> (press the button, make sure everyone knows the sound), <b>check the batteries</b>, and <b>talk through your family's escape plan</b> — two ways out of every room, one meeting spot outside.</p>
<p>Here's our favorite story of the week: at Fort Carson, firefighters are riding along with Domino's pizza deliveries Tuesday through Thursday — and if your smoke alarms pass a quick on-the-spot check, <b>the whole order is free</b>. One little beep, one free pizza, one safer family. That's what this week is about.</p>
<p>The Fire Babys fight fires in the game. This week, you get to be the hero in real life. <i>the future is BLAZING FOR FIRE BABYS 🔥</i></p>
