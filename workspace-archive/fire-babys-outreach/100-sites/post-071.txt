71. Electrical Outlet Safety: What real firefighters want you to know.
Category: FIREFIGHTER WISDOM
Date: October 5, 2026

Ask any firefighter and they'll tell you: One plug per outlet for big appliances. Don't force plugs that don't fit, and never remove the third prong. If an outlet sparks, smells hot, or has black marks — stop using it and tell a grown-up. Cover unused outlets if toddlers are around. They've seen what happens when families know this — and when they don't. The difference is practice.Firefighters train for this every single day. Now your family can too: One plug per outlet for big appliances. Don't force plugs that don't fit, and never remove the third prong. If an outlet sparks, smells hot, or has black marks — stop using it and tell a grown-up. Cover unused outlets if toddlers are around. Respect to the crews who run toward what everyone else runs from.

REAL WORDS FROM REAL FIREFIGHTERS:
"If the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your way out. Call 911 from outside your home."
— South Beach Regional Fire Authority, WA
