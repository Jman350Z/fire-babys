77. Holiday Decoration Safety: Why this game exists.
Category: MISSION
Date: October 5, 2026

This is exactly why Fire Babys exists. Holiday lights are festive — and a fire risk. Check old strings for cracked wires, don't link more strands than the box allows, and turn everything off before bed. Keep trees watered (a dry tree burns in seconds) and decorations 3 feet from heat.
