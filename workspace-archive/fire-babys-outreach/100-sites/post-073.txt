73. Holiday Decoration Safety: A practical guide for grown-ups.
Category: FOR PARENTS
Date: October 5, 2026

Here's how to teach it at home: Holiday lights are festive — and a fire risk. Check old strings for cracked wires, don't link more strands than the box allows, and turn everything off before bed. Keep trees watered (a dry tree burns in seconds) and decorations 3 feet from heat. Make it a conversation, not a lecture — kids remember what they practice, not what they're told.
