89. Get Low Under Smoke: Written straight to young readers.
Category: FOR KIDS
Date: October 5, 2026

Hey, future fire-safety hero! Here's something super important: Smoke rises — the cleanest air is near the floor. If there's smoke, get low and go: crawl on your hands and knees under the smoke to your exit. Before opening any door, feel it with the back of your hand. Hot door? Use your second way out.
