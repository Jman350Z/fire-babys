95. Never Hide During a Fire: How Fire Babys teaches this for real.
Category: IN THE GAME
Date: October 5, 2026

In Fire Babys, this lesson is baked right into the fun. Kids' instinct is to hide — under beds, in closets. Teach them the opposite: get OUT and stay out. Firefighters in gear can look scary; show kids pictures so they know the helpers. Once you're at the meeting spot, never go back inside for toys or pets.
