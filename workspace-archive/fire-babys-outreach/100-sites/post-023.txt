23. Matches and Lighters Out of Reach: A practical guide for grown-ups.
Category: FOR PARENTS
Date: October 5, 2026

Here's how to teach it at home: Kids are naturally curious about fire. Store matches and lighters up high in a locked cabinet — never in a drawer a child can reach. Tell kids: if you find matches or a lighter, tell a grown-up right away. Don't touch, don't hide it. Make it a conversation, not a lecture — kids remember what they practice, not what they're told.
