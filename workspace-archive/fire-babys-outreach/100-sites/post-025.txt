25. Matches and Lighters Out of Reach: How Fire Babys teaches this for real.
Category: IN THE GAME
Date: October 5, 2026

In Fire Babys, this lesson is baked right into the fun. Kids are naturally curious about fire. Store matches and lighters up high in a locked cabinet — never in a drawer a child can reach. Tell kids: if you find matches or a lighter, tell a grown-up right away. Don't touch, don't hide it.
