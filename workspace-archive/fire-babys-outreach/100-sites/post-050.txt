50. What To Do If Clothes Catch Fire: How Fire Babys teaches this for real.
Category: IN THE GAME
Date: October 5, 2026

In Fire Babys, this lesson is baked right into the fun. Don't run — running feeds the flames with air. STOP, DROP, and ROLL. Shout for help while you roll. Once the flames are out, cool the burn with cool (not cold) water and get help. This is the single most important fire skill a child can learn.
