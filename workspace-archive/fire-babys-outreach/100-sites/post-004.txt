4. Stop, Drop, and Roll: Written straight to young readers.
Category: FOR KIDS
Date: October 5, 2026

Hey, future fire-safety hero! Here's something super important: If your clothes catch fire: STOP where you are, DROP to the ground, and ROLL back and forth to smother the flames. Cover your face with your hands while you roll. Practice it like a game so your body remembers it in a scary moment. Pretty cool, right? Knowing this stuff makes YOU the safety expert in your family.
