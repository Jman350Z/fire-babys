99. Kitchen Burn Prevention: Written straight to young readers.
Category: FOR KIDS
Date: October 5, 2026

Hey, future fire-safety hero! Here's something super important: Turn pot handles inward so little hands can't grab them. Keep a 3-foot kid-free zone around the stove. Test bath and food temperature before kids touch. Hot liquids cause the worst childhood burns — keep coffee and soup away from table edges. Pretty cool, right?
