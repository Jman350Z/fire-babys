21. Never Leave Cooking Unattended: What real firefighters want you to know.
Category: FIREFIGHTER WISDOM
Date: October 5, 2026

Ask any firefighter and they'll tell you: Cooking causes nearly half of all home fires. Stay in the kitchen when you fry, boil, or broil. If you must leave, turn the burner off first. Keep a lid nearby — sliding it over a pan fire smothers it. Never throw water on a grease fire. They've seen what happens when families know this — and when they don't.
