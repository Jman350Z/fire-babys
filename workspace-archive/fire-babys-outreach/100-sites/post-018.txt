18. Never Leave Cooking Unattended: A practical guide for grown-ups.
Category: FOR PARENTS
Date: October 5, 2026

Here's how to teach it at home: Cooking causes nearly half of all home fires. Stay in the kitchen when you fry, boil, or broil. If you must leave, turn the burner off first. Keep a lid nearby — sliding it over a pan fire smothers it. Never throw water on a grease fire. Make it a conversation, not a lecture — kids remember what they practice, not what they're told.
