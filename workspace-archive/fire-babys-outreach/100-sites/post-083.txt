83. Know Your Fire Extinguisher: PASS: A practical guide for grown-ups.
Category: FOR PARENTS
Date: October 5, 2026

Here's how to teach it at home: For small fires, remember PASS: Pull the pin, Aim at the base of the fire, Squeeze the handle, Sweep side to side. Aim at the BASE — not the flames. And know when to walk away: if the fire is bigger than a wastebasket, get out and call 911. Make it a conversation, not a lecture — kids remember what they practice, not what they're told.
