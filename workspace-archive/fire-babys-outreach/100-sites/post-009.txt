9. Test Your Smoke Alarms Monthly: Written straight to young readers.
Category: FOR KIDS
Date: October 5, 2026

Hey, future fire-safety hero! Here's something super important: Smoke alarms save lives — but only if they work. Press the test button once a month, change batteries once a year (or get a 10-year sealed alarm), and replace the whole alarm every 10 years. Put one inside every bedroom, outside each sleeping area, and on every level of your home. Pretty cool, right? Knowing this stuff makes YOU the safety expert in your family.
