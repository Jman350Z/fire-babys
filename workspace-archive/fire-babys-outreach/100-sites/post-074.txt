74. Holiday Decoration Safety: Written straight to young readers.
Category: FOR KIDS
Date: October 5, 2026

Hey, future fire-safety hero! Here's something super important: Holiday lights are festive — and a fire risk. Check old strings for cracked wires, don't link more strands than the box allows, and turn everything off before bed. Keep trees watered (a dry tree burns in seconds) and decorations 3 feet from heat. Pretty cool, right? Knowing this stuff makes YOU the safety expert in your family.
