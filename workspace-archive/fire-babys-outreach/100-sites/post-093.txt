93. Never Hide During a Fire: A practical guide for grown-ups.
Category: FOR PARENTS
Date: October 5, 2026

Here's how to teach it at home: Kids' instinct is to hide — under beds, in closets. Teach them the opposite: get OUT and stay out. Firefighters in gear can look scary; show kids pictures so they know the helpers. Once you're at the meeting spot, never go back inside for toys or pets. Make it a conversation, not a lecture — kids remember what they practice, not what they're told.
