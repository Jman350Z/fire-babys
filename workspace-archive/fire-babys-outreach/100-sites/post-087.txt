87. Know Your Fire Extinguisher: PASS: Why this game exists.
Category: MISSION
Date: October 5, 2026

This is exactly why Fire Babys exists. For small fires, remember PASS: Pull the pin, Aim at the base of the fire, Squeeze the handle, Sweep side to side. Aim at the BASE — not the flames. And know when to walk away: if the fire is bigger than a wastebasket, get out and call 911.
