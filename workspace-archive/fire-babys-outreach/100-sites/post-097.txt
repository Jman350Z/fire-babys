97. Never Hide During a Fire: Why this game exists.
Category: MISSION
Date: October 5, 2026

This is exactly why Fire Babys exists. Kids' instinct is to hide — under beds, in closets. Teach them the opposite: get OUT and stay out. Firefighters in gear can look scary; show kids pictures so they know the helpers. Once you're at the meeting spot, never go back inside for toys or pets. REAL WORDS FROM REAL FIREFIGHTERS:
