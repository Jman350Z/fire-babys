41. Don't Overload Extension Cords: What real firefighters want you to know.
Category: FIREFIGHTER WISDOM
Date: October 5, 2026

Ask any firefighter and they'll tell you: Extension cords are for temporary use, not permanent wiring. Never plug a big appliance into a power strip or extension cord, and never run cords under rugs where heat builds up. If a cord feels hot, unplug it and tell a grown-up. They've seen what happens when families know this — and when they don't. The difference is practice.Firefighters train for this every single day. Now your family can too: Extension cords are for temporary use, not permanent wiring. REAL WORDS FROM REAL FIREFIGHTERS:
