28. Space Heaters Need Space: A practical guide for grown-ups.
Category: FOR PARENTS
Date: October 5, 2026

Here's how to teach it at home: Give space heaters 3 feet of clearance on every side — away from beds, curtains, papers, and little hands. Plug them directly into the wall, never into an extension cord or power strip. Turn them off when you leave the room or go to sleep. Make it a conversation, not a lecture — kids remember what they practice, not what they're told.
