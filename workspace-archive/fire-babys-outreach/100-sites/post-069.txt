69. Electrical Outlet Safety: Written straight to young readers.
Category: FOR KIDS
Date: October 5, 2026

Hey, future fire-safety hero! Here's something super important: One plug per outlet for big appliances. Don't force plugs that don't fit, and never remove the third prong. If an outlet sparks, smells hot, or has black marks — stop using it and tell a grown-up. Cover unused outlets if toddlers are around. Pretty cool, right?
