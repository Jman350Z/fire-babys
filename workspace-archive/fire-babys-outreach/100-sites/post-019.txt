19. Never Leave Cooking Unattended: Written straight to young readers.
Category: FOR KIDS
Date: October 5, 2026

Hey, future fire-safety hero! Here's something super important: Cooking causes nearly half of all home fires. Stay in the kitchen when you fry, boil, or broil. If you must leave, turn the burner off first. Keep a lid nearby — sliding it over a pan fire smothers it. Never throw water on a grease fire. Pretty cool, right? REAL WORDS FROM REAL FIREFIGHTERS:
"If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home."
— National Fire Protection Association (NFPA)
