34. Close Your Bedroom Door at Night: Written straight to young readers.
Category: FOR KIDS
Date: October 5, 2026

Hey, future fire-safety hero! Here's something super important: A closed door can save your life in a fire. It slows smoke and flames, buying precious minutes. Make 'close before you doze' a family rule. Firefighters say the difference between an open and closed door is the difference between life and death. Pretty cool, right?
