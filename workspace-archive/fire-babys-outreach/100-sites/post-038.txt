38. Don't Overload Extension Cords: A practical guide for grown-ups.
Category: FOR PARENTS
Date: October 5, 2026

Here's how to teach it at home: Extension cords are for temporary use, not permanent wiring. Never plug a big appliance into a power strip or extension cord, and never run cords under rugs where heat builds up. If a cord feels hot, unplug it and tell a grown-up. Make it a conversation, not a lecture — kids remember what they practice, not what they're told.
