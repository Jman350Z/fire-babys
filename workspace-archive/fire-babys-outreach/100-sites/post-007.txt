7. Stop, Drop, and Roll: Why this game exists.
Category: MISSION
Date: October 5, 2026

This is exactly why Fire Babys exists. If your clothes catch fire: STOP where you are, DROP to the ground, and ROLL back and forth to smother the flames. Cover your face with your hands while you roll. Practice it like a game so your body remembers it in a scary moment.
