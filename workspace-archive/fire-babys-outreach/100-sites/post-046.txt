46. Candle Safety: What real firefighters want you to know.
Category: FIREFIGHTER WISDOM
Date: October 5, 2026

Ask any firefighter and they'll tell you: A candle is an open flame in your home. Keep candles 12 inches from anything that can burn, never leave them burning unattended, and blow them out before bed. Better yet: use flameless LED candles — same cozy glow, zero risk. They've seen what happens when families know this — and when they don't. The difference is practice.Firefighters train for this every single day. Now your family can too: A candle is an open flame in your home. Keep candles 12 inches from anything that can burn, never leave them burning unattended, and blow them out before bed. Better yet: use flameless LED candles — same cozy glow, zero risk. Respect to the crews who run toward what everyone else runs from.

REAL WORDS FROM REAL FIREFIGHTERS:
"In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside."
— Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL
