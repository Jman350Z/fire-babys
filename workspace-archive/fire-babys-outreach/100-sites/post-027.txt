27. Matches and Lighters Out of Reach: Why this game exists.
Category: MISSION
Date: October 5, 2026

This is exactly why Fire Babys exists. Kids are naturally curious about fire. Store matches and lighters up high in a locked cabinet — never in a drawer a child can reach. Tell kids: if you find matches or a lighter, tell a grown-up right away. Don't touch, don't hide it.
