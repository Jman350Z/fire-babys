82. Grill Safety: Why this game exists.
Category: MISSION
Date: October 5, 2026

This is exactly why Fire Babys exists. Grills belong outside, 3 feet from the house, railings, and overhangs. Never grill in a garage — carbon monoxide kills silently. Keep kids and pets 3 feet away from the hot zone. Clean the grease tray: built-up grease is fuel for flare-ups.
