94. Never Hide During a Fire: Written straight to young readers.
Category: FOR KIDS
Date: October 5, 2026

Hey, future fire-safety hero! Here's something super important: Kids' instinct is to hide — under beds, in closets. Teach them the opposite: get OUT and stay out. Firefighters in gear can look scary; show kids pictures so they know the helpers. Once you're at the meeting spot, never go back inside for toys or pets. Pretty cool, right? REAL WORDS FROM REAL FIREFIGHTERS:
"In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside."
