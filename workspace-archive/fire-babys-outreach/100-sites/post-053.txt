53. Calling 911: When and How: A practical guide for grown-ups.
Category: FOR PARENTS
Date: October 5, 2026

Here's how to teach it at home: Teach kids to call 911 for real emergencies: fire, someone hurt, danger. Practice saying your full home address out loud — dispatchers need it fast. Teach them: stay on the line, answer questions, and never hang up first. And never call 911 as a joke. Make it a conversation, not a lecture — kids remember what they practice, not what they're told.
