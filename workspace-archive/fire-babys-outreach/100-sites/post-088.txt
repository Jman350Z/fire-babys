88. Get Low Under Smoke: A practical guide for grown-ups.
Category: FOR PARENTS
Date: October 5, 2026

Here's how to teach it at home: Smoke rises — the cleanest air is near the floor. If there's smoke, get low and go: crawl on your hands and knees under the smoke to your exit. Before opening any door, feel it with the back of your hand. Hot door? Use your second way out. Make it a conversation, not a lecture — kids remember what they practice, not what they're told.
