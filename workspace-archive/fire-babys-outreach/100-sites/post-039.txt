39. Don't Overload Extension Cords: Written straight to young readers.
Category: FOR KIDS
Date: October 5, 2026

Hey, future fire-safety hero! Here's something super important: Extension cords are for temporary use, not permanent wiring. Never plug a big appliance into a power strip or extension cord, and never run cords under rugs where heat builds up. If a cord feels hot, unplug it and tell a grown-up. Pretty cool, right?
