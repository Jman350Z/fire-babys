68. Electrical Outlet Safety: A practical guide for grown-ups.
Category: FOR PARENTS
Date: October 5, 2026

Here's how to teach it at home: One plug per outlet for big appliances. Don't force plugs that don't fit, and never remove the third prong. If an outlet sparks, smells hot, or has black marks — stop using it and tell a grown-up. Cover unused outlets if toddlers are around. Make it a conversation, not a lecture — kids remember what they practice, not what they're told.
