86. Know Your Fire Extinguisher: PASS: What real firefighters want you to know.
Category: FIREFIGHTER WISDOM
Date: October 5, 2026

Ask any firefighter and they'll tell you: For small fires, remember PASS: Pull the pin, Aim at the base of the fire, Squeeze the handle, Sweep side to side. Aim at the BASE — not the flames. And know when to walk away: if the fire is bigger than a wastebasket, get out and call 911. They've seen what happens when families know this — and when they don't. The difference is practice.Firefighters train for this every single day. Now your family can too: For small fires, remember PASS: Pull the pin, Aim at the base of the fire, Squeeze the handle, Sweep side to side. Aim at the BASE — not the flames. And know when to walk away: if the fire is bigger than a wastebasket, get out and call 911. Respect to the crews who run toward what everyone else runs from.

REAL WORDS FROM REAL FIREFIGHTERS:
"Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things. Smoke is poisonous. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your exit."
— U.S. Fire Administration (USFA)
