54. Calling 911: When and How: Written straight to young readers.
Category: FOR KIDS
Date: October 5, 2026

Hey, future fire-safety hero! Here's something super important: Teach kids to call 911 for real emergencies: fire, someone hurt, danger. Practice saying your full home address out loud — dispatchers need it fast. Teach them: stay on the line, answer questions, and never hang up first. And never call 911 as a joke. Pretty cool, right?
