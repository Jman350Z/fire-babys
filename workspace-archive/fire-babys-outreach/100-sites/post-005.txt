5. Stop, Drop, and Roll: How Fire Babys teaches this for real.
Category: IN THE GAME
Date: October 5, 2026

In Fire Babys, this lesson is baked right into the fun. If your clothes catch fire: STOP where you are, DROP to the ground, and ROLL back and forth to smother the flames. Cover your face with your hands while you roll. Practice it like a game so your body remembers it in a scary moment.
