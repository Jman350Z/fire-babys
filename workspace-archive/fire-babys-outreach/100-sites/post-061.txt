61. Practice Your Escape Plan: What real firefighters want you to know.
Category: FIREFIGHTER WISDOM
Date: October 5, 2026

Ask any firefighter and they'll tell you: Draw your home's floor plan with your kids and mark two exits from every room. Pick a family meeting spot outside — a tree, a mailbox, a neighbor's porch. Practice the drill twice a year. In a real fire, you won't have time to think — only to move. They've seen what happens when families know this — and when they don't. The difference is practice.Firefighters train for this every single day. Now your family can too: Draw your home's floor plan with your kids and mark two exits from every room. Pick a family meeting spot outside — a tree, a mailbox, a neighbor's porch. Practice the drill twice a year. In a real fire, you won't have time to think — only to move. Respect to the crews who run toward what everyone else runs from.

REAL WORDS FROM REAL FIREFIGHTERS:
"If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home."
— National Fire Protection Association (NFPA)
