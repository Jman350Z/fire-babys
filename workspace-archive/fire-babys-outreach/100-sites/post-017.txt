17. Two Ways Out of Every Room: Why this game exists.
Category: MISSION
Date: October 5, 2026

This is exactly why Fire Babys exists. Every room needs two ways out — usually a door and a window. Walk your home with your kids and find both exits for every room. Make sure windows open easily and screens come off fast. In a fire, your first choice might be blocked by smoke.
