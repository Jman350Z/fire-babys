81. Grill Safety: What real firefighters want you to know.
Category: FIREFIGHTER WISDOM
Date: October 5, 2026

Ask any firefighter and they'll tell you: Grills belong outside, 3 feet from the house, railings, and overhangs. Never grill in a garage — carbon monoxide kills silently. Keep kids and pets 3 feet away from the hot zone. Clean the grease tray: built-up grease is fuel for flare-ups. They've seen what happens when families know this — and when they don't. The difference is practice.Firefighters train for this every single day. Now your family can too: Grills belong outside, 3 feet from the house, railings, and overhangs. Never grill in a garage — carbon monoxide kills silently. Keep kids and pets 3 feet away from the hot zone. Clean the grease tray: built-up grease is fuel for flare-ups. Respect to the crews who run toward what everyone else runs from.

REAL WORDS FROM REAL FIREFIGHTERS:
"Get out, stay out and stay alive! Crawl low under smoke — smoke rises, so cleaner, cooler air is near the floor. Get down on your hands and knees and crawl low under the smoke to the nearest exit."
— Lynbrook Fire Department, NY
