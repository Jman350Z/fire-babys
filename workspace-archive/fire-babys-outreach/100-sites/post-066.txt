66. Campfire Safety: What real firefighters want you to know.
Category: FIREFIGHTER WISDOM
Date: October 5, 2026

Ask any firefighter and they'll tell you: Keep campfires small and 25 feet from tents and trees. Never leave a fire unattended — not even for a minute. Drown it, stir it, feel it: pour water on, stir the ashes, and make sure it's cold before you walk away. If it's too hot to touch, it's too hot to leave. They've seen what happens when families know this — and when they don't. The difference is practice.Firefighters train for this every single day. Now your family can too: Keep campfires small and 25 feet from tents and trees. Never leave a fire unattended — not even for a minute. Drown it, stir it, feel it: pour water on, stir the ashes, and make sure it's cold before you walk away. If it's too hot to touch, it's too hot to leave. Respect to the crews who run toward what everyone else runs from.

REAL WORDS FROM REAL FIREFIGHTERS:
"When the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke."
