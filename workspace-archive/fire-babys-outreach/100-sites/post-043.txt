43. Candle Safety: A practical guide for grown-ups.
Category: FOR PARENTS
Date: October 5, 2026

Here's how to teach it at home: A candle is an open flame in your home. Keep candles 12 inches from anything that can burn, never leave them burning unattended, and blow them out before bed. Better yet: use flameless LED candles — same cozy glow, zero risk. Make it a conversation, not a lecture — kids remember what they practice, not what they're told.
