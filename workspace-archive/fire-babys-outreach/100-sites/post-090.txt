90. Get Low Under Smoke: How Fire Babys teaches this for real.
Category: IN THE GAME
Date: October 5, 2026

In Fire Babys, this lesson is baked right into the fun. Smoke rises — the cleanest air is near the floor. If there's smoke, get low and go: crawl on your hands and knees under the smoke to your exit. Before opening any door, feel it with the back of your hand. Hot door? Use your second way out.
