58. Practice Your Escape Plan: A practical guide for grown-ups.
Category: FOR PARENTS
Date: October 5, 2026

Here's how to teach it at home: Draw your home's floor plan with your kids and mark two exits from every room. Pick a family meeting spot outside — a tree, a mailbox, a neighbor's porch. Practice the drill twice a year. In a real fire, you won't have time to think — only to move. Make it a conversation, not a lecture — kids remember what they practice, not what they're told.
