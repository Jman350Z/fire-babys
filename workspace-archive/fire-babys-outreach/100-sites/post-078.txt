78. Grill Safety: A practical guide for grown-ups.
Category: FOR PARENTS
Date: October 5, 2026

Here's how to teach it at home: Grills belong outside, 3 feet from the house, railings, and overhangs. Never grill in a garage — carbon monoxide kills silently. Keep kids and pets 3 feet away from the hot zone. Clean the grease tray: built-up grease is fuel for flare-ups. Make it a conversation, not a lecture — kids remember what they practice, not what they're told.
