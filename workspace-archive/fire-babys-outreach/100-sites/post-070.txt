70. Electrical Outlet Safety: How Fire Babys teaches this for real.
Category: IN THE GAME
Date: October 5, 2026

In Fire Babys, this lesson is baked right into the fun. One plug per outlet for big appliances. Don't force plugs that don't fit, and never remove the third prong. If an outlet sparks, smells hot, or has black marks — stop using it and tell a grown-up. Cover unused outlets if toddlers are around.
