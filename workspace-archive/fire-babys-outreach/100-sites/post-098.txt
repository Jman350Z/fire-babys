98. Kitchen Burn Prevention: A practical guide for grown-ups.
Category: FOR PARENTS
Date: October 5, 2026

Here's how to teach it at home: Turn pot handles inward so little hands can't grab them. Keep a 3-foot kid-free zone around the stove. Test bath and food temperature before kids touch.
