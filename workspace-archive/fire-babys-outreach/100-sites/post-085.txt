85. Know Your Fire Extinguisher: PASS: How Fire Babys teaches this for real.
Category: IN THE GAME
Date: October 5, 2026

In Fire Babys, this lesson is baked right into the fun. For small fires, remember PASS: Pull the pin, Aim at the base of the fire, Squeeze the handle, Sweep side to side. Aim at the BASE — not the flames. And know when to walk away: if the fire is bigger than a wastebasket, get out and call 911. REAL WORDS FROM REAL FIREFIGHTERS:
"If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home."
