76. Holiday Decoration Safety: What real firefighters want you to know.
Category: FIREFIGHTER WISDOM
Date: October 5, 2026

Ask any firefighter and they'll tell you: Holiday lights are festive — and a fire risk. Check old strings for cracked wires, don't link more strands than the box allows, and turn everything off before bed. Keep trees watered (a dry tree burns in seconds) and decorations 3 feet from heat. They've seen what happens when families know this — and when they don't. The difference is practice.Firefighters train for this every single day. Now your family can too: Holiday lights are festive — and a fire risk. Check old strings for cracked wires, don't link more strands than the box allows, and turn everything off before bed. Keep trees watered (a dry tree burns in seconds) and decorations 3 feet from heat. Respect to the crews who run toward what everyone else runs from.

REAL WORDS FROM REAL FIREFIGHTERS:
"In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside."
— Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL
