56. Calling 911: When and How: What real firefighters want you to know.
Category: FIREFIGHTER WISDOM
Date: October 5, 2026

Ask any firefighter and they'll tell you: Teach kids to call 911 for real emergencies: fire, someone hurt, danger. Practice saying your full home address out loud — dispatchers need it fast. Teach them: stay on the line, answer questions, and never hang up first. And never call 911 as a joke. They've seen what happens when families know this — and when they don't. The difference is practice.Firefighters train for this every single day. Now your family can too: Teach kids to call 911 for real emergencies: fire, someone hurt, danger.
