52. What To Do If Clothes Catch Fire: Why this game exists.
Category: MISSION
Date: October 5, 2026

This is exactly why Fire Babys exists. Don't run — running feeds the flames with air. STOP, DROP, and ROLL. Shout for help while you roll. Once the flames are out, cool the burn with cool (not cold) water and get help. This is the single most important fire skill a child can learn.
