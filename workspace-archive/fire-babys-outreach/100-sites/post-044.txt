44. Candle Safety: Written straight to young readers.
Category: FOR KIDS
Date: October 5, 2026

Hey, future fire-safety hero! Here's something super important: A candle is an open flame in your home. Keep candles 12 inches from anything that can burn, never leave them burning unattended, and blow them out before bed. Better yet: use flameless LED candles — same cozy glow, zero risk.
