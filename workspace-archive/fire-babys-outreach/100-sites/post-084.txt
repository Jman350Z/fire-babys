84. Know Your Fire Extinguisher: PASS: Written straight to young readers.
Category: FOR KIDS
Date: October 5, 2026

Hey, future fire-safety hero! Here's something super important: For small fires, remember PASS: Pull the pin, Aim at the base of the fire, Squeeze the handle, Sweep side to side. Aim at the BASE — not the flames. And know when to walk away: if the fire is bigger than a wastebasket, get out and call 911. Pretty cool, right? Knowing this stuff makes YOU the safety expert in your family.
