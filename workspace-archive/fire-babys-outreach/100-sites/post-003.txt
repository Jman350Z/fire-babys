3. Stop, Drop, and Roll: A practical guide for grown-ups.
Category: FOR PARENTS
Date: October 5, 2026

Here's how to teach it at home: If your clothes catch fire: STOP where you are, DROP to the ground, and ROLL back and forth to smother the flames. Cover your face with your hands while you roll. Practice it like a game so your body remembers it in a scary moment. Make it a conversation, not a lecture — kids remember what they practice, not what they're told.
