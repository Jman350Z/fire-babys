29. Space Heaters Need Space: Written straight to young readers.
Category: FOR KIDS
Date: October 5, 2026

Hey, future fire-safety hero! Here's something super important: Give space heaters 3 feet of clearance on every side — away from beds, curtains, papers, and little hands. Plug them directly into the wall, never into an extension cord or power strip. Turn them off when you leave the room or go to sleep. Pretty cool, right? Knowing this stuff makes YOU the safety expert in your family.
