91. Get Low Under Smoke: What real firefighters want you to know.
Category: FIREFIGHTER WISDOM
Date: October 5, 2026

Ask any firefighter and they'll tell you: Smoke rises — the cleanest air is near the floor. If there's smoke, get low and go: crawl on your hands and knees under the smoke to your exit. Before opening any door, feel it with the back of your hand. Hot door? Use your second way out. They've seen what happens when families know this — and when they don't. The difference is practice.Firefighters train for this every single day. Now your family can too: Smoke rises — the cleanest air is near the floor. If there's smoke, get low and go: crawl on your hands and knees under the smoke to your exit. Before opening any door, feel it with the back of your hand. Hot door? Use your second way out. Respect to the crews who run toward what everyone else runs from.

REAL WORDS FROM REAL FIREFIGHTERS:
"If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home."
— National Fire Protection Association (NFPA)
