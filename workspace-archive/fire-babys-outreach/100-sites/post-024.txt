24. Matches and Lighters Out of Reach: Written straight to young readers.
Category: FOR KIDS
Date: October 5, 2026

Hey, future fire-safety hero! Here's something super important: Kids are naturally curious about fire. Store matches and lighters up high in a locked cabinet — never in a drawer a child can reach. Tell kids: if you find matches or a lighter, tell a grown-up right away. Don't touch, don't hide it. Pretty cool, right? Knowing this stuff makes YOU the safety expert in your family.
