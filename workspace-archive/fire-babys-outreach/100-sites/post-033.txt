33. Close Your Bedroom Door at Night: A practical guide for grown-ups.
Category: FOR PARENTS
Date: October 5, 2026

Here's how to teach it at home: A closed door can save your life in a fire. It slows smoke and flames, buying precious minutes. Make 'close before you doze' a family rule. Firefighters say the difference between an open and closed door is the difference between life and death. Make it a conversation, not a lecture — kids remember what they practice, not what they're told.
