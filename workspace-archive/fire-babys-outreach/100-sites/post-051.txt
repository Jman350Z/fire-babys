51. What To Do If Clothes Catch Fire: What real firefighters want you to know.
Category: FIREFIGHTER WISDOM
Date: October 5, 2026

Ask any firefighter and they'll tell you: Don't run — running feeds the flames with air. STOP, DROP, and ROLL. Shout for help while you roll. Once the flames are out, cool the burn with cool (not cold) water and get help. This is the single most important fire skill a child can learn. They've seen what happens when families know this — and when they don't. The difference is practice.Firefighters train for this every single day. REAL WORDS FROM REAL FIREFIGHTERS:
"Get out, stay out and stay alive! Crawl low under smoke — smoke rises, so cleaner, cooler air is near the floor. Get down on your hands and knees and crawl low under the smoke to the nearest exit."
— Lynbrook Fire Department, NY
