26. Matches and Lighters Out of Reach: What real firefighters want you to know.
Category: FIREFIGHTER WISDOM
Date: October 5, 2026

Ask any firefighter and they'll tell you: Kids are naturally curious about fire. Store matches and lighters up high in a locked cabinet — never in a drawer a child can reach. Tell kids: if you find matches or a lighter, tell a grown-up right away. Don't touch, don't hide it. They've seen what happens when families know this — and when they don't. The difference is practice.Firefighters train for this every single day. Now your family can too: Kids are naturally curious about fire. REAL WORDS FROM REAL FIREFIGHTERS:
"Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things. Smoke is poisonous. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your exit."
