8. Test Your Smoke Alarms Monthly: A practical guide for grown-ups.
Category: FOR PARENTS
Date: October 5, 2026

Here's how to teach it at home: Smoke alarms save lives — but only if they work. Press the test button once a month, change batteries once a year (or get a 10-year sealed alarm), and replace the whole alarm every 10 years. Put one inside every bedroom, outside each sleeping area, and on every level of your home. Make it a conversation, not a lecture — kids remember what they practice, not what they're told.
