72. Electrical Outlet Safety: Why this game exists.
Category: MISSION

This is exactly why Fire Babys exists. One plug per outlet for big appliances. Don't force plugs that don't fit, and never remove the third prong. If an outlet sparks, smells hot, or has black marks — stop using it and tell a grown-up. Cover unused outlets if toddlers are around.
