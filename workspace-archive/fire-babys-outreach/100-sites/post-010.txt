Category: IN THE GAME

In Fire Babys, this lesson is baked right into the fun. Smoke alarms save lives — but only if they work. Press the test button once a month, change batteries once a year (or get a 10-year sealed alarm), and replace the whole alarm every 10 years. Put one inside every bedroom, outside each sleeping area, and on every level of your home.
