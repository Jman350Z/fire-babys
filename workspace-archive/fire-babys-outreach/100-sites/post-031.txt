31. Space Heaters Need Space: What real firefighters want you to know.
Category: FIREFIGHTER WISDOM
Date: October 5, 2026

Ask any firefighter and they'll tell you: Give space heaters 3 feet of clearance on every side — away from beds, curtains, papers, and little hands. Plug them directly into the wall, never into an extension cord or power strip. Turn them off when you leave the room or go to sleep. They've seen what happens when families know this — and when they don't. The difference is practice.Firefighters train for this every single day. Now your family can too: Give space heaters 3 feet of clearance on every side — away from beds, curtains, papers, and little hands. Plug them directly into the wall, never into an extension cord or power strip. Turn them off when you leave the room or go to sleep. Respect to the crews who run toward what everyone else runs from.

REAL WORDS FROM REAL FIREFIGHTERS:
"If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home."
— National Fire Protection Association (NFPA)
