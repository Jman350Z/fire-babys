75. Holiday Decoration Safety: How Fire Babys teaches this for real.
Category: IN THE GAME
Date: October 5, 2026

In Fire Babys, this lesson is baked right into the fun. Holiday lights are festive — and a fire risk. Check old strings for cracked wires, don't link more strands than the box allows, and turn everything off before bed. Keep trees watered (a dry tree burns in seconds) and decorations 3 feet from heat.
