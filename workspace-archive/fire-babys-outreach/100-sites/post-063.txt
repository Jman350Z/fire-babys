63. Campfire Safety: A practical guide for grown-ups.
Category: FOR PARENTS
Date: October 5, 2026

Here's how to teach it at home: Keep campfires small and 25 feet from tents and trees. Never leave a fire unattended — not even for a minute. Drown it, stir it, feel it: pour water on, stir the ashes, and make sure it's cold before you walk away. If it's too hot to touch, it's too hot to leave. Make it a conversation, not a lecture — kids remember what they practice, not what they're told.
