6. Stop, Drop, and Roll: What real firefighters want you to know.
Category: FIREFIGHTER WISDOM
Date: October 5, 2026

Ask any firefighter and they'll tell you: If your clothes catch fire: STOP where you are, DROP to the ground, and ROLL back and forth to smother the flames. Cover your face with your hands while you roll. Practice it like a game so your body remembers it in a scary moment. They've seen what happens when families know this — and when they don't. The difference is practice.Firefighters train for this every single day.
