14. Two Ways Out of Every Room: Written straight to young readers.
Category: FOR KIDS
Date: October 5, 2026

Hey, future fire-safety hero! Here's something super important: Every room needs two ways out — usually a door and a window. Walk your home with your kids and find both exits for every room. Make sure windows open easily and screens come off fast. In a fire, your first choice might be blocked by smoke. Pretty cool, right? Knowing this stuff makes YOU the safety expert in your family. REAL WORDS FROM REAL FIREFIGHTERS:
"Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things. Smoke is poisonous. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your exit."
— U.S. Fire Administration (USFA)
