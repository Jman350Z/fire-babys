67. Campfire Safety: Why this game exists.
Category: MISSION
Date: October 5, 2026

This is exactly why Fire Babys exists. Keep campfires small and 25 feet from tents and trees. Never leave a fire unattended — not even for a minute. Drown it, stir it, feel it: pour water on, stir the ashes, and make sure it's cold before you walk away. If it's too hot to touch, it's too hot to leave.
