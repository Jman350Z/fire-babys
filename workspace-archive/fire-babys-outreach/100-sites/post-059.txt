59. Practice Your Escape Plan: Written straight to young readers.
Category: FOR KIDS
Date: October 5, 2026

Hey, future fire-safety hero! Here's something super important: Draw your home's floor plan with your kids and mark two exits from every room. Pick a family meeting spot outside — a tree, a mailbox, a neighbor's porch. Practice the drill twice a year. In a real fire, you won't have time to think — only to move. Pretty cool, right?
