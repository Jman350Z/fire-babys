64. Campfire Safety: Written straight to young readers.
Category: FOR KIDS
Date: October 5, 2026

Hey, future fire-safety hero! Here's something super important: Keep campfires small and 25 feet from tents and trees. Never leave a fire unattended — not even for a minute. Drown it, stir it, feel it: pour water on, stir the ashes, and make sure it's cold before you walk away. If it's too hot to touch, it's too hot to leave. Pretty cool, right? REAL WORDS FROM REAL FIREFIGHTERS:
"In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside."
