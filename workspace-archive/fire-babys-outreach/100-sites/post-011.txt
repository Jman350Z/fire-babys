11. Test Your Smoke Alarms Monthly: What real firefighters want you to know.
Category: FIREFIGHTER WISDOM
Date: October 5, 2026

Ask any firefighter and they'll tell you: Smoke alarms save lives — but only if they work. Press the test button once a month, change batteries once a year (or get a 10-year sealed alarm), and replace the whole alarm every 10 years. Put one inside every bedroom, outside each sleeping area, and on every level of your home. They've seen what happens when families know this — and when they don't. The difference is practice.Firefighters train for this every single day. Now your family can too: Smoke alarms save lives — but only if they work.
