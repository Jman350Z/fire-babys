13. Two Ways Out of Every Room: A practical guide for grown-ups.
Category: FOR PARENTS
Date: October 5, 2026

Here's how to teach it at home: Every room needs two ways out — usually a door and a window. Walk your home with your kids and find both exits for every room. Make sure windows open easily and screens come off fast. In a fire, your first choice might be blocked by smoke. Make it a conversation, not a lecture — kids remember what they practice, not what they're told.
