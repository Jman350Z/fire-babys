16. Two Ways Out of Every Room: What real firefighters want you to know.
Category: FIREFIGHTER WISDOM
Date: October 5, 2026

Ask any firefighter and they'll tell you: Every room needs two ways out — usually a door and a window. Walk your home with your kids and find both exits for every room. Make sure windows open easily and screens come off fast. In a fire, your first choice might be blocked by smoke. They've seen what happens when families know this — and when they don't. The difference is practice.Firefighters train for this every single day. Now your family can too: Every room needs two ways out — usually a door and a window. Walk your home with your kids and find both exits for every room. Make sure windows open easily and screens come off fast. In a fire, your first choice might be blocked by smoke. Respect to the crews who run toward what everyone else runs from.

REAL WORDS FROM REAL FIREFIGHTERS:
"In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside."
— Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL
