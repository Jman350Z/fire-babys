79. Grill Safety: Written straight to young readers.
Category: FOR KIDS
Date: October 5, 2026

Hey, future fire-safety hero! Here's something super important: Grills belong outside, 3 feet from the house, railings, and overhangs. Never grill in a garage — carbon monoxide kills silently. Keep kids and pets 3 feet away from the hot zone. Clean the grease tray: built-up grease is fuel for flare-ups. Pretty cool, right? Knowing this stuff makes YOU the safety expert in your family. REAL WORDS FROM REAL FIREFIGHTERS:
"If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home."
— National Fire Protection Association (NFPA)
