96. Never Hide During a Fire: What real firefighters want you to know.
Category: FIREFIGHTER WISDOM
Date: October 5, 2026

Ask any firefighter and they'll tell you: Kids' instinct is to hide — under beds, in closets. Teach them the opposite: get OUT and stay out. Firefighters in gear can look scary; show kids pictures so they know the helpers. Once you're at the meeting spot, never go back inside for toys or pets. They've seen what happens when families know this — and when they don't. The difference is practice.Firefighters train for this every single day. Now your family can too: Kids' instinct is to hide — under beds, in closets. Teach them the opposite: get OUT and stay out. Respect to the crews who run toward what everyone else runs from.
