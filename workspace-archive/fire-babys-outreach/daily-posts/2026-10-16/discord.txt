🔋 CHARGING SAFETY WEEK — DAY 4 (Discord)

Day 4: KEEP THE CHARGING ZONE CLEAR.

The rules: charge on a clear, dry surface, keep drinks and water far away from cords and devices, and never pile papers, clothes, toys, or blankets on or around something that's charging. Heat needs room to escape.

Today's server challenge: with a grown-up, clear your family's charging spot until only the device and its cord are left. Done? Post a 🔋 — never share personal info or addresses.

The pros: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)
