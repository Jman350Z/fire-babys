🔋 CHARGING SAFETY WEEK — DAY 4: The Clear Zone Rule

A charging device needs breathing room. Papers, clothes, toys, and blankets piled on or around it can trap heat, and a drink spilled near cords is a hazard all on its own.

The Clear Zone Rule is simple: charge on a clear, dry surface, keep water far away, and never stack anything on top of a charging phone, tablet, or e-bike battery. If a device ever feels hot, smells strange, or looks swollen, a grown-up unplugs it if it's safe and takes it out of service.

The pros: "Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things. Smoke is poisonous. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your exit." — U.S. Fire Administration (USFA) (Source: https://www.safeoutcome.com/documents/00120/USFA%20Escape%20Planning.pdf)
