🔋 CHARGING SAFETY WEEK — DAY 4: A Letter to Grown-Ups

Dear Fan Club grown-ups,

Day 4 is the tidy one: keep the charging zone clear. The corner where phones and tablets charge quietly collects papers, laundry, toys, and coffee cups — and a charging device buried under clutter can't shed heat, while a spilled drink near live cords is its own danger.

Tonight's two-minute fix: pick the family charging spot, clear everything off it except the devices and cords, and move drinks to a different surface for good. Clear, dry, and boring is exactly what safe charging looks like.

The pros: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)
