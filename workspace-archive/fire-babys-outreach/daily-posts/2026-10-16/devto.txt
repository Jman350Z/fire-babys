🔋 CHARGING SAFETY WEEK — DAY 4: Designing the Clear Zone

Today's game-design note: clutter is a readable game state. In Fire Babys levels we can show a charging station crowded with papers, toys, and a water cup — and let players clear it, item by item, until the zone reads clean: clear surface, dry, nothing stacked on the device.

Kids learn the rule by doing it: a charging device needs open space around it so heat can escape, and water never belongs near cords. Spatial cleanup puzzles teach that faster than any warning label.

The pros: "Get out, stay out and stay alive! Crawl low under smoke — smoke rises, so cleaner, cooler air is near the floor. Get down on your hands and knees and crawl low under the smoke to the nearest exit." — Lynbrook Fire Department, NY (Source: http://lynbrookfd.org/beta05/fire-prevention.html)
