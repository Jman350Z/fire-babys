🔋 CHARGING SAFETY WEEK — DAY 4 🔋

Day 4: KEEP THE CHARGING ZONE CLEAR.

Blaze, Ruko & Cinder cleared their charging counter today — papers moved away, the water cup moved far from the cords, and nothing stacked on top of the phone or tablet while they charged. A clear, dry charging zone gives heat room to escape and keeps water where it belongs: far away.

Today's family mission: with a grown-up, clear ONE charging spot — no papers, clothes, toys, or drinks piled around it.

The pros: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)
