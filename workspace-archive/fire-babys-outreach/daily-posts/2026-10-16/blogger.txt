🔋 CHARGING SAFETY WEEK — DAY 4: "Blaze and the Clear Counter"

Blaze looked at the charging counter and sighed. Papers leaned on the tablet. A toy sat on the phone cord. A cup of water stood way too close.

"Charging needs a clear zone," Blaze said. So the Fire Babys tidied together: papers to the desk, toy to the toy box, water to the other counter. Soon only the phone and tablet remained, charging on a clear, dry surface with room to breathe.

"Clear, dry, and nothing on top," said Ruko. "That's the rule."

The pros: "When the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke." — Champaign Fire Department, IL (Source: https://champaignil.gov/2022/10/05/champaign-fire-department-celebrates-the-100th-anniversary-of-fire-prevention-week/)
