🔋 CHARGING SAFETY WEEK — DAY 4: The Clear Zone Checklist

Day 4 of Charging Safety Week is all about what surrounds a charging device.

✅ Charge on a clear, dry surface
✅ Keep drinks and water far away from cords
✅ Never stack papers, clothes, or toys on a charging device
✅ Leave open space around it so heat can escape
✅ Hot, strange-smelling, or swollen device? A grown-up unplugs it if safe — and it's out of service

The pros: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)
