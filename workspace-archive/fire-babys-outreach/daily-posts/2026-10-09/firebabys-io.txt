Kids Lead the Safety Walk

Today's new Fire Babys picture shows Blaze, Ruko, and Cinder at their meeting-place tree with a family safety checklist — and your family can copy them in about two minutes. Start at the front door, look in every room together, and let the kids lead: Where are the two ways out? Is the path clear? Where do we meet outside?

Kids remember what they get to lead. Point at the smoke alarm, name your outside meeting tree or mailbox out loud, and finish your walk right at that meeting place. End with a cheer, not a scare — practice should feel like a team win.

The South Beach Regional Fire Authority puts it simply: "If the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out." — South Beach Regional Fire Authority, WA (Source: https://www.sbrfa.org/fire-escape-planning)

Fire Babys games teach fire safety through play, and game proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day. No partnerships exist yet — that's the mission we're building toward, one family safety walk at a time.
