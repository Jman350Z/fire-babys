🔥 CHARGE INTO SAFETY FRIDAY 🔥

Families, let's talk batteries the Fire Babys way. Phones, tablets, scooters, and toy batteries charge safest on a hard, flat surface — never on a bed, couch, or pile of clothes. Grown-ups do the charging, plugs come out when the battery is full, and charging stays out of bedrooms while kids sleep.

Make it a tonight mission: walk through your home with your kids, move chargers to a clear counter spot, and check that nothing hot or charging is buried under blankets. Little habits like that prevent big scares.

The NFPA's rule is worth memorizing: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)
