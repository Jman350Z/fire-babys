Fire Prevention Week is wrapping up, so here's our favorite lesson from the week: the safest fire plan is the one your family has actually practiced out loud. Two ways out of every room, one outside meeting place everyone can name, and a quick drill that ends with everybody together at that spot.

This week our Fire Babys practiced right alongside you — Blaze held the meeting-place sign, Ruko carried the checklist, and Cinder brought the flashlight. That's exactly how practice should feel for kids: clear, calm, and a little bit proud at the end.

The NFPA's rule stays on our fridge all year: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)
