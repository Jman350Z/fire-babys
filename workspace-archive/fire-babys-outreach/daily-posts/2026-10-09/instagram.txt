🔥 GLOW SAFE, NOT FLAME RISKY 🔥

Spooky season is coming, so let's glow the safe way. Real candles are grown-up only, stay at least a foot away from anything that can burn, and never stay lit in an empty room. For kids, costumes, and pumpkins, flameless candles and glow sticks give you all the glow with none of the flame.

Try this at home: swap one real candle for a flameless one tonight and let your kids be the official "glow keepers." Decorating together is the fun part — staying safe is how we keep it fun.

The Champaign Fire Department reminds every family: "When the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out." — Champaign Fire Department, IL (Source: https://champaignil.gov/2022/10/05/champaign-fire-department-celebrates-the-100th-anniversary-of-fire-prevention-week/)
