Quick 911 practice that every kid can learn today 🧡

Step 1: know your home address out loud — street, number, and town. Step 2: in a real emergency, get outside first, go to your meeting place, and call 911 from outside or a neighbor's home. Never call from inside a smoky house, and never go back in.

Turn it into a 30-second game tonight: "What's our address? Where do we meet? Who do we call once we're out?" Fast answers now mean calm answers later.

The U.S. Fire Administration's rule: "Get out fast and stay out." — U.S. Fire Administration (USFA) (Source: https://www.safeoutcome.com/documents/00120/USFA%20Escape%20Planning.pdf)
