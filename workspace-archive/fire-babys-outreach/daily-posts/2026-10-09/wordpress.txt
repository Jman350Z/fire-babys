Cooler Nights, Safer Heat: The 3-Foot Rule

As evenings get cooler, space heaters and fireplaces come back out — and they need a clear safety zone. Keep kids, pets, bedding, curtains, and furniture at least 3 feet away from any heater or fireplace. That 3-foot circle is a kid-free, clutter-free zone, every single night.

Two more heater habits matter: plug space heaters directly into a wall outlet (never an extension cord), and switch them off before bed or whenever you leave the room. Grown-ups own the heater controls; kids own the job of keeping the safety circle clear.

The Champaign Fire Department's reminder belongs in every season: "When the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out." — Champaign Fire Department, IL (Source: https://champaignil.gov/2022/10/05/champaign-fire-department-celebrates-the-100th-anniversary-of-fire-prevention-week/)
