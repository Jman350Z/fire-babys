Building kid-safe by design, not by patch notes

When we design Fire Babys games, safety rules come before features. No chat or messaging, no gambling or loot boxes, no unreviewed outside links, and characters who are always loved and never harmed. If a mechanic can't pass that checklist, it doesn't ship — no matter how fun the prototype felt.

That same design discipline shows up in the gameplay itself: hazards are puzzles to prevent and escape, never jokes at a character's expense. Kids practice spotting risks, choosing a safe way out, and helping their squad — the exact habits real safety lessons try to build.

The Plainfield Fire Protection District teaches the core move in five words: "In a fire: get low and go." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)

Fire Babys proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day. No partnerships exist yet — we're building the games, and the safety curriculum inside them, first.
