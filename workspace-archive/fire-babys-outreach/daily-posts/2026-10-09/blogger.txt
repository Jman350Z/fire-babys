The Great Pillow-Fort Fire Drill

On rainy afternoons, our favorite classroom game starts with a pillow fort and a blanket held just high enough to crawl under. "Smoke stays up, safe air stays down," we say, and everybody giggles their way — low, low, low — across the rug to the door. Nobody is scared, because it's pretend, and everybody is learning, because their bodies remember the game.

At the door we practice the next move: feel it first with the back of your hand. Cool door? Open it slowly and go. Pretend-hot door? Giggles again — we pick our second way out instead. Then it's a race-walk (never a run) to the big tree outside, our meeting place, where the whole crew checks in together.

The South Beach Regional Fire Authority's rule ties the whole story together: "If the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out." — South Beach Regional Fire Authority, WA (Source: https://www.sbrfa.org/fire-escape-planning)
