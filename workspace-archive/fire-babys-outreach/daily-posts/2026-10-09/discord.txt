🔥 WEEKEND CHALLENGE, FIRE BABYS SQUAD 🔥

Your mission this weekend: make your family's meeting-place sign. Draw it, paint it, or build it from craft supplies — a big friendly sign for the tree, mailbox, or corner where everyone meets after getting out. Then walk the whole route together and finish at your sign.

Drop your sign design (or your best meeting-place emoji map 🗺️🌳📍) in the replies and tell us how fast your family made it outside in practice. Fastest isn't the prize — everybody knowing the plan is.
