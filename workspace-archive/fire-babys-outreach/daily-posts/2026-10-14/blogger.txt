Blaze and the Frayed Cord — Charging Safety Week, Day 2

Ruko found a charging cord behind the couch. It was bent, cracked, and the wires were peeking out.

"Still works!" he said.

Blaze shook his head. "Frayed means unsafe. A damaged cord doesn't get one more charge — it gets recycled, and a grown-up gets a new one."

Cinder checked the list: use the right charger, check cords, unplug when full. The phone on the table beeped at 100%, and Blaze unplugged it with a smile.

Three checks, one safe charging station.
