🔋 CHARGING SAFETY WEEK — DAY 2 🔋

Day 2 mission for families: use the right charger, check every cord, and unplug when it's full.

Blaze, Ruko & Cinder did a charging-station check today. A frayed cord went straight in the recycle bin — frayed = unsafe, no exceptions. The phone at 100%? Unplugged.

Try it at home tonight: look at every charging cord in the house with a grown-up. Cracked, chewed, or frayed? It goes. Only use the charger made for your device.
