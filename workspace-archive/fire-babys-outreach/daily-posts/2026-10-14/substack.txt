Charging Safety Week, Day 2 — A Letter to Grown-Ups: Check the Cords

Dear Fan Club,

Day 2 is the unglamorous one that matters: chargers and cords.

Most homes have a drawer of mystery chargers and at least one cord that's fraying at the neck. Kids plug into whatever fits. Today's Fire Babys mission is simple — use the charger made for the device, retire any cord that's cracked or frayed, and unplug devices when they hit 100% instead of leaving them on charge all day and night.

Make it a two-minute family check tonight. Let your kids be the inspectors — they love finding the bad cord.

The pros: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)

Fire Babys games teach fire safety through play; proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day. No partnerships yet — that's the mission we're building toward.
