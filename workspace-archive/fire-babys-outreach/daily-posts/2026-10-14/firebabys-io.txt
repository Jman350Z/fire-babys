Charging Safety Week — Day 2: The Right Charger Rule

Day 2 of Charging Safety Week is about what you plug in — and when you unplug it.

Use the charger that was made for your device. Check every cord before you use it: if it's frayed, cracked, or chewed, don't use it — show a grown-up and recycle it. And when a device reaches 100%, unplug it instead of leaving it charging for hours.

In today's concept art, Blaze unplugs a fully charged phone, Ruko drops a frayed cord in the recycle bin, and Cinder checks off the Day 2 list: use the right charger, check cords, unplug when full.
