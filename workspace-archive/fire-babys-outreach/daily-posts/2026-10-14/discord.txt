Day 2: THE RIGHT CHARGER + CORD CHECK.

The rules: use the charger made for your device, check cords for cracks or frays, and unplug when the battery hits 100%. A frayed cord is never "still fine" — it goes in the recycle bin and a grown-up replaces it.

Today's server challenge: do a cord check at home with a grown-up. Found a damaged cord? Post a 🔋 when it's safely out of use — never share personal info or addresses.
