Charging Safety Week, Day 2: Your Cord-Check Checklist

Day 2 of Charging Safety Week focuses on chargers and cords — the parts of charging families look at least.

The checklist: 1) Use the charger made for your device. 2) Inspect every cord — frayed, cracked, or chewed means stop using it, recycle it, and let a grown-up replace it. 3) Unplug at 100% instead of leaving devices charging indefinitely. 4) Keep spare chargers stored neatly, not tangled in a drawer where cords bend and break.
