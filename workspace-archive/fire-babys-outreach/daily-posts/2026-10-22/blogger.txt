Blaze and the Low Road Out — a Get Low and Go story

The practice smoke curled along the ceiling of West Ember High's hallway, soft and silvery and pretend. "Smoke takes the high road," said Blaze, dropping to hands and knees with a grin. "So we take the low road!"

Ruko tied his bandana, pointed down the clear hallway to the open door, and crawled beside him. At the bedroom door, Cinder stopped, pressed the back of her hand to the wood, and nodded. "Cool. This way's safe today — but if it ever feels hot, we don't open it. We take our second way out."

Under the pretend smoke they went, low and laughing, out the door and across the grass to the big meeting-place tree, where the whole crew tumbled in for a group cheer. Nobody went back inside — not for a toy, not for anything. Once you're out, you stay out.

The firefighters of Lynbrook, NY teach the real version: "Get out, stay out and stay alive! Crawl low under smoke — smoke rises, so cleaner, cooler air is near the floor." — Lynbrook Fire Department, NY (Source: http://lynbrookfd.org/beta05/fire-prevention.html)
