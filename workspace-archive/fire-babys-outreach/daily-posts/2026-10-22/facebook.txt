🔥 GET LOW AND GO THURSDAY 🔥

Family drill day! Smoke rises, so the cleaner, cooler air stays down near the floor. If you ever have to move through smoke, the move is simple: get down on your hands and knees and crawl low under the smoke to your nearest exit — then stay out.

Blaze, Ruko & Cinder practiced it today as a hallway race to the open door and the meeting-place tree. Before any door, feel it first — if a door is hot, that way is closed; use your second way out instead.

The pros: "Get out, stay out and stay alive! Crawl low under smoke — smoke rises, so cleaner, cooler air is near the floor. Get down on your hands and knees and crawl low under the smoke to the nearest exit." — Lynbrook Fire Department, NY (Source: http://lynbrookfd.org/beta05/fire-prevention.html)
