Crawl low, stay safe ✅ Smoke rises, clean air stays low ✅ Feel doors first ✅ Out to the meeting place

Smoke is poisonous, and it climbs. Down low is where the breathable air lives — that's why firefighters teach every family the same move: get low and go under the smoke to your exit, and once you're out, stay out.

The pros: "Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things. Smoke is poisonous. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your exit." — U.S. Fire Administration (USFA) (Source: https://www.safeoutcome.com/documents/00120/USFA%20Escape%20Planning.pdf)
