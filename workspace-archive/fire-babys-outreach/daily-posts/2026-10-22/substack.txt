GET LOW AND GO THURSDAY — a letter to grown-ups

Dear Fan Club,

Today's ten-minute family drill is the simplest one we teach, and it might be the most important: get low and go. Smoke rises and carries the worst of a fire with it, so the cleaner, cooler air in a smoky room is down near the floor. If your family ever has to escape through smoke, hands and knees, low under the smoke, straight to the nearest exit is the move that matters.

Make it a game tonight. Everyone crawls the hallway once — slowly, laughing, the way Blaze, Ruko & Cinder do in today's concept art — and finishes at your outside meeting place. Teach the door check on the way: feel the door first, and if it's hot, that exit is closed and the second way out is the plan.

The pros say it plainly: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)

Fire Babys games teach fire safety through play for everyone. Game proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day — no partnerships exist yet; that's the mission we're building toward, together.
