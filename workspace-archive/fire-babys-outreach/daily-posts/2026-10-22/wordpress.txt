Get Low and Go Thursday — the full family checklist

Smoke rises. That single fact is why one of the most important escape skills is also the simplest: when smoke is in the way, get down low, where the cleaner, cooler air is, and crawl under the smoke to your nearest exit.

Today's checklist for every family:
1. Know the move: hands and knees, low under smoke, straight to the nearest exit.
2. Feel every door before opening it — if a door is hot, use your second way out.
3. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets, or things.
4. Meet at your one outside meeting place, and grown-ups call 911 from outside.

The South Beach Regional Fire Authority teaches it this way: "If the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your way out. Call 911 from outside your home." — South Beach Regional Fire Authority, WA (Source: https://www.sbrfa.org/fire-escape-planning)
