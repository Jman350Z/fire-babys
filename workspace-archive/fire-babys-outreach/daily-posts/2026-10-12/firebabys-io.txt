The 60-Second Smoke Alarm Test (Today's Art: Blaze on Duty)

1) Stand under each smoke alarm. Press the TEST button — don't use a real flame or smoke.
2) Listen for the loud beep. No sound? Swap the battery first, then the whole alarm (replace alarms every 10 years).
3) Mark your calendar. Test monthly; this Monday is your new reminder day.

Blaze learned the hard way: an alarm you never test is a decoration, not a lifesaver. Ruko keeps a checklist like the one in today's picture — alarms on every level of the home, inside each bedroom, and outside every sleeping area.

The pros: "Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things. Smoke is poisonous. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your exit." — U.S. Fire Administration (USFA) (Source: https://www.safeoutcome.com/documents/00120/USFA%20Escape%20Planning.pdf)
