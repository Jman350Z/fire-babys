A Monday letter to the grown-ups,

Blaze woke up this morning and climbed a ladder — not for adventure, but to press a tiny button on the ceiling. That button is the loudest guardian your home owns.

Here's the routine real firefighters want in every home: test each alarm once a month (press TEST, don't light anything), replace batteries yearly, and replace the whole unit every 10 years. Put alarms inside every bedroom, outside sleeping areas, and on every level. Kids who hear the alarm sound during a calm test won't freeze when it sounds for real.

Do it with your kids watching. Let them press the button. A family that practices calm stays calm.

The pros: "Get out, stay out and stay alive! Crawl low under smoke — smoke rises, so cleaner, cooler air is near the floor. Get down on your hands and knees and crawl low under the smoke to the nearest exit." — Lynbrook Fire Department, NY (Source: http://lynbrookfd.org/beta05/fire-prevention.html)

Fire Babys games teach fire safety through play; proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day. No partnerships yet — that's the mission.
