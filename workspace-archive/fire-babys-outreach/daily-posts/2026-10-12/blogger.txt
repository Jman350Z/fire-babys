Blaze and the BEEP

Monday morning. Blaze climbed the ladder, pressed the button, and — BEEP! — the house rang like a tiny thunderstorm. Ruko marked the first checkbox on his clipboard with the seriousness of a general. Cinder covered her ears and laughed, because loud is funny when you're safe.

That's the whole story, and it's the whole lesson: a smoke alarm you test is a guardian. One you never press is a guess.

Make it a family ritual. Monthly, press the button together. Yearly, fresh batteries. Every decade, a new alarm. It's the cheapest, loudest lifesaver you'll ever own.

The pros: "When the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke." — Champaign Fire Department, IL (Source: https://champaignil.gov/2022/10/05/champaign-fire-department-celebrates-the-100th-anniversary-of-fire-prevention-week/)
