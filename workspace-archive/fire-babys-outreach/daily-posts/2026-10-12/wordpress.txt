The Monthly Smoke Alarm Checklist (Safety Check Monday Edition)

Walk through this today and you'll be done before your coffee cools:

☐ Test every alarm — press TEST, listen for the beep.
☐ Replace any dead battery on the spot; no "I'll do it later."
☐ Count your alarms: one on every level, inside each bedroom, outside sleeping areas. Missing one? That's today's errand.
☐ Check the date on each alarm — replace the whole unit if it's 10+ years old.

Alarms don't prevent fires. They buy you the minutes that everything else depends on — the get-low-and-go minutes, the two-ways-out minutes, the get-to-the-meeting-place minutes.

The pros: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)
