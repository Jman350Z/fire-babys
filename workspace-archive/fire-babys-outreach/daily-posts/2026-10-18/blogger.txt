Blaze and the Warning-Signs Poster — Charging Safety Week, Day 6

Blaze noticed the poster first. It hung by the door at Fire Baby headquarters, five bright icons in a row: a thermometer, a puffed-up battery, a wiggly smell cloud, a hissing sound, and a puff of smoke. "Those are the warning signs," Cinder said, tapping the clipboard. "If a charging gadget shows even one, we don't touch it. Not ever."

Ruko practiced the three steps out loud so he would never forget: Don't touch it. Tell a grown-up. Get outside. Then the three friends marched — at a safe, silly, wiggly walk — to their meeting place by the mailbox, just to prove they knew the way.

The pros: "Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things. Smoke is poisonous. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your exit." — U.S. Fire Administration (USFA) (Source: https://www.safeoutcome.com/documents/00120/USFA%20Escape%20Planning.pdf)
