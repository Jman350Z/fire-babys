Day 6: KNOW THE WARNING SIGNS.

A charging device is trying to tell you something if it gets very hot, looks swollen, smells strange, hisses, or starts to smoke. Blaze, Ruko & Cinder's rule is simple: don't touch it, tell a grown-up right away, and get outside. Kids never try to fix, move, or unplug a device that is showing warning signs.

Today's family mission: with a grown-up, learn the five warning signs together — too hot, swollen, strange smell, hissing, smoke — and point to your home's outside meeting place. Knowing the signs before anything happens is what keeps everybody calm and safe.

The pros: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)
