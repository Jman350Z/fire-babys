Day 6: KNOW THE WARNING SIGNS.

Blaze, Ruko & Cinder spent today learning the poster every family should know: a charging device that is very hot, swollen, smells strange, hisses, or smokes is showing a warning sign. The kid rule never changes: don't touch it, tell a grown-up, get outside.

Today's server challenge: name all five warning signs from memory — hot, swollen, strange smell, hissing, smoke — and drop a ⚠️ when you can. Bonus: tell us (no personal info, no addresses) where your family's outside meeting place is — front yard, sidewalk, or a neighbor's porch.

The pros: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)
