Charging Safety Week — Day 6: The Warning-Signs Checklist

Day 6 turns five warning signs into a checklist any family can learn in two minutes. A charging device needs grown-up help right away if it is: very hot to be near, swollen or bulging, giving off a strange smell, hissing, or smoking.

The kid checklist is just as short: ☐ Don't touch it ☐ Tell a grown-up ☐ Get outside to the family meeting place. Grown-ups: a device showing these signs should not be charged or used again, and smoke or fire always means get out first and call 911 from outside.

The pros: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)
