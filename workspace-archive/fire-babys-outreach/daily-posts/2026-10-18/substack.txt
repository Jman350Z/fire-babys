Charging Safety Week — Day 6: A Letter to Grown-Ups About Warning Signs

Dear Fan Club grown-ups,

Kids are often the first to notice when something is wrong — a phone that feels hotter than usual, a tablet case that looks puffed up, a strange sweet or chemical smell near a charger. Day 6 teaches them what to do with that observation: never touch it, tell you immediately, and head outside. Your job is the calm second half: keep everyone out, and call 911 from outside if there is smoke or fire.

Take two minutes tonight to walk the five signs together — too hot, swollen, strange smell, hissing, smoke — and agree on your outside meeting place. A damaged or swollen battery should stop being used and be handled only by grown-ups following local disposal guidance.

The pros: "Get out, stay out and stay alive! Crawl low under smoke — smoke rises, so cleaner, cooler air is near the floor. Get down on your hands and knees and crawl low under the smoke to the nearest exit." — Lynbrook Fire Department, NY (Source: http://lynbrookfd.org/beta05/fire-prevention.html)
