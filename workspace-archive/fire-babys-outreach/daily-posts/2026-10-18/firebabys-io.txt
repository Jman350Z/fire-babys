CHARGING SAFETY WEEK — DAY 6: The Warning-Signs Rule

Most charging is safe. Day 6 is about the rare moment it is not — and how a kid who knows the signs can act fast and stay safe. The five warning signs are: a device or battery that is very hot, swollen or bulging, giving off a strange smell, hissing, or smoking.

The rule has three steps and no exceptions for kids: 1) Don't touch it — never pick it up, move it, or try to unplug it. 2) Tell a grown-up immediately. 3) Get outside to your family's meeting place, and grown-ups call 911 from outside if there is smoke or fire.

The pros: "Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things. Smoke is poisonous. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your exit." — U.S. Fire Administration (USFA) (Source: https://www.safeoutcome.com/documents/00120/USFA%20Escape%20Planning.pdf)
