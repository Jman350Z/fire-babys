Charging Safety Week — Day 7 Finale: Designing a Pledge Kids Actually Keep

For the Day 7 finale of Charging Safety Week, the Fire Babys design question was: how do you make six separate safety rules survive after the week ends? The answer is a pledge mechanic — Blaze, Ruko & Cinder stand on tiered steps, check off each rule on one big poster (hard flat surface; right charger, check cords; stay nearby with a grown-up; clear and dry zone; big batteries outside; know the warning signs), and the player wins by reciting the full set in order.

That structure matters for kid-safe design. A checklist that lives in one place is recallable in a stressful moment in a way six scattered lessons are not, and the fantasy stays empowering — kids are the heroes who remembered everything — while the real-world actions stay exactly what firefighters ask for.

The pros: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)
