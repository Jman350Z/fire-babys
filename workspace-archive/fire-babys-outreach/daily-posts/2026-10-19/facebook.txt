Day 7: TAKE THE CHARGING SAFETY PLEDGE.

All week Blaze, Ruko & Cinder learned how to charge safely — and today they graduate. The pledge is the whole week in six lines: charge on a hard, flat surface; use the right charger and check cords; stay nearby with a grown-up; keep the zone clear and dry; charge big batteries outside, away from doors; and know the warning signs — if it is very hot, swollen, smells strange, hisses, or smokes, don't touch it, tell a grown-up, get outside.

Today's family mission: say the pledge together out loud, then walk to your outside meeting place one more time to celebrate. You did the work all week — this is the part where it becomes a habit.

The pros: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)
