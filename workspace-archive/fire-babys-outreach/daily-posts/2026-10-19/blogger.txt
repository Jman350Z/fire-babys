Blaze and the Charging Safety Pledge — Charging Safety Week, Day 7 Finale

On the last morning of Charging Safety Week, Blaze stood on the middle step so everyone could see. "We learned six things," Blaze said, holding up the big pledge poster. "Can we say them all without peeking?" Ruko went first, pointing at each check mark: hard flat surface, right charger and checked cords, stay nearby with a grown-up.

Cinder finished the list — clear and dry zone, big batteries outside, know the warning signs — and the whole crowd cheered and threw confetti. Then, still giggling, all three walked out the clear front door to their meeting place by the mailbox, just to prove the pledge worked with their feet, not just their words.
