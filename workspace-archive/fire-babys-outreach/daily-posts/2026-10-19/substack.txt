Charging Safety Week — Day 7 Finale: A Letter to Grown-Ups About the Pledge

Dear Fan Club grown-ups,

We made it to the finale. Over six days your kids learned where to charge, which charger to use, why cords get checked, why a grown-up stays nearby, why the zone stays clear and dry, why big batteries live outside, and what the warning signs look like. Day 7 turns all of that into one family pledge — short enough to say at the kitchen table, strong enough to stick.

Say it together tonight, then do the celebration walk: out the door, down the path, to your outside meeting place. That walk, done calmly and with a little confetti energy, is the rehearsal that matters if an alarm ever sounds for real.

Thank you for spending this week with Blaze, Ruko & Cinder. Charging Safety Week ends today — the safe habits do not.
