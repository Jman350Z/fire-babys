Charging Safety Week — Day 7 Finale: The Charging Safety Pledge Checklist

Day 7 collects the whole week into one checklist any family can post by the charging spot. ☐ Charge on a hard, flat surface — never on beds, couches, or under pillows ☐ Use the right charger, check cords, unplug when full ☐ Charge where a grown-up can see it and stay nearby ☐ Keep the charging zone clear and dry, nothing stacked on top ☐ Charge big batteries outside, away from doors and exits ☐ Know the warning signs — very hot, swollen, strange smell, hissing, or smoke means don't touch it, tell a grown-up, get outside.

Finish the week the Fire Babys way: say the pledge together, then practice your walk to your outside meeting place. Grown-ups: if there is ever smoke or fire, get everyone out first and call 911 from outside.

The pros: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)
