CHARGING SAFETY WEEK — DAY 7 FINALE: The Charging Safety Pledge

Charging Safety Week ends with the lesson that holds all the others together: a simple pledge any family can say in under a minute. Charge on a hard, flat surface — never on a bed, couch, or under a pillow. Use the right charger, check cords, and unplug when full. Charge where a grown-up can see it and stay nearby. Keep the charging zone clear and dry, with nothing stacked on top. Charge big batteries like e-bikes and scooters outside, away from doors and exits. And know the warning signs: very hot, swollen, strange smell, hissing, or smoke means don't touch it, tell a grown-up, get outside.

The finale practice is celebration with a purpose: say the pledge together, then walk your escape route to your outside meeting place. Repeating the safe steps when everything is calm is what makes them automatic when it counts.

The pros: "Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things. Smoke is poisonous. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your exit." — U.S. Fire Administration (USFA) (Source: https://www.safeoutcome.com/documents/00120/USFA%20Escape%20Planning.pdf)
