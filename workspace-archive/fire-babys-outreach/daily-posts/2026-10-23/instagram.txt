🌳 MEETING PLACE FRIDAY 🌳

Pick your spot ✅ Big tree, mailbox, or neighbor's porch ✅ Walk to it together ✅ Stay there and count everyone ✅

One outside meeting place means nobody has to guess where anyone is. Go straight there, stay there, and let a grown-up call 911 from outside.

The pros: "Get out, stay out and stay alive! Crawl low under smoke — smoke rises, so cleaner, cooler air is near the floor. Get down on your hands and knees and crawl low under the smoke to the nearest exit." — Lynbrook Fire Department, NY (Source: http://lynbrookfd.org/beta05/fire-prevention.html)
