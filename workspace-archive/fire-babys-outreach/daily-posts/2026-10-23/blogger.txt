🌳 MEETING PLACE FRIDAY — Blaze and the Big Tree 🌳

Blaze had a problem: every practice drill ended with Fire Babys in three different places. Ruko was by the gate, Cinder was on the porch, and nobody could tell if the whole crew was safe.

So they chose one spot together — the big tree out front, where everyone can be seen and counted. They walked to it from the kitchen, then from the bedrooms, until the path felt easy. Now the rule is simple: out, straight to the tree, stay at the tree.

The pros: "Get out, stay out and stay alive! Crawl low under smoke — smoke rises, so cleaner, cooler air is near the floor. Get down on your hands and knees and crawl low under the smoke to the nearest exit." — Lynbrook Fire Department, NY (Source: http://lynbrookfd.org/beta05/fire-prevention.html)

Fire Babys games teach fire safety through play for everyone; proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day. No partnerships yet — that's the mission we're building toward.
