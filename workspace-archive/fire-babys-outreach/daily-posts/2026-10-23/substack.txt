🌳 MEETING PLACE FRIDAY — A Letter to Grown-Ups 🌳

Dear Fan Club families,

If your household had to get out tonight, would everyone end up in the same place — or scattered around the yard looking for each other? A single, agreed outside meeting place removes that guesswork in the moment it matters most.

Tonight's ten-minute task: choose the spot together (a tree, mailbox, or neighbor's porch in front of the home), walk to it from the bedrooms, agree that everyone stays there, and practice who calls 911 from outside. Small, calm practice now is what makes a stressful moment orderly later.

The pros: "If the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your way out. Call 911 from outside your home." — South Beach Regional Fire Authority, WA — via the NFPA wording in our quote bank (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)

Fire Babys games teach fire safety through play for everyone; proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day. No partnerships yet — that's the mission we're building toward.
