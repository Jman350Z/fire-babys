🌳 MEETING PLACE FRIDAY — The Full Family Checklist 🌳

Use this checklist with a grown-up today: ☐ Pick ONE outside meeting place (tree, mailbox, or neighbor's porch) in front of the home. ☐ Make sure everyone in the household can name it without prompting. ☐ Walk to it from two different rooms. ☐ Agree: once you're out, you stay out and stay at the spot. ☐ Decide which grown-up calls 911 from outside. ☐ Practice it twice a year.

A meeting place only works if it's the same spot every time — changing it mid-emergency is what causes families to go back searching for each other.

The pros: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)
