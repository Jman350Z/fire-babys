🌳 MEETING PLACE FRIDAY 🌳

Every family needs ONE outside meeting place — a big tree, a mailbox, or a neighbor's porch — far enough from the house that it's safe, and close enough that everyone can find it fast, day or night.

Today's family mission: pick your spot together, walk to it from two different rooms, and make it a rule — once you're out, you go straight there, you stay there, and a grown-up counts everyone. Blaze, Ruko & Cinder picked the big tree out front.

The pros: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)

Fire Babys games teach fire safety through play for everyone; proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day. No partnerships yet — that's the mission we're building toward.
