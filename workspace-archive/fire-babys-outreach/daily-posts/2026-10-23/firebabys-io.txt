🌳 MEETING PLACE FRIDAY — Why One Spot Outside Saves Confusion 🌳

In an emergency, the hardest part isn't getting out — it's knowing everyone else got out too. That's why firefighters ask every household to choose a single outside meeting place before anything ever happens.

Pick a landmark that never moves: a tree, a streetlight, a mailbox. Make sure it's in front of the home so firefighters can see you, tell every household member, and practice walking there. Once you arrive, stay put — wandering back to look for someone puts a second person at risk.

The pros: "Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things. Smoke is poisonous. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your exit." — U.S. Fire Administration (USFA) (Source: https://www.safeoutcome.com/documents/00120/USFA%20Escape%20Planning.pdf)
