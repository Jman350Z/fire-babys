Stop, Drop and Roll Sunday — The Full Family Checklist

Some safety rules are short enough for a three-year-old and important enough for everyone. Stop, Drop and Roll is the classic. Here is today's family checklist:

1. STOP — If clothes catch fire, stop right where you are. Never run; running fans the flames.
2. DROP — Drop to the ground and cover your face with your hands.
3. ROLL — Roll back and forth, over and over, until the flames are completely out.
4. COOL AND CALL — Cool any burn with clean, cool (not cold) water, and get medical help. Call 911 in an emergency.
5. PRACTICE — Rehearse the three moves on a soft rug today, the way the Fire Babys crew does in today's concept art, so the reflex is ready before it is ever needed.

The pros: "When the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke." — Champaign Fire Department, IL (Source: https://champaignil.gov/2022/10/05/champaign-fire-department-celebrates-the-100th-anniversary-of-fire-prevention-week/)
