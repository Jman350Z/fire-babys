Stop, Drop and Roll Sunday

Today on firebabys.io we are teaching the oldest drill in fire safety — the three moves to make if clothes ever catch fire.

STOP where you are. Running feeds flames with air, so the first move is to freeze. DROP to the ground and cover your face with your hands. ROLL back and forth until the flames are out — then cool any burn with clean, cool water and get help.

Today's concept art shows the drill the Fire Babys way: a happy practice session on a living-room rug, Blaze calling the stop, Cinder cheering beside the safety checklist, and a whole tiered crowd of Fire Babys applauding. Practice like this — calm, playful, repeated — is how a slogan becomes a reflex.

The pros: "When the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke." — Champaign Fire Department, IL (Source: https://champaignil.gov/2022/10/05/champaign-fire-department-celebrates-the-100th-anniversary-of-fire-prevention-week/)
