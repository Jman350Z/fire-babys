The Day the Whole Crew Learned to Roll

Blaze raised one hand in the middle of the living room. "STOP!" he called — and everybody froze, right where they stood, because that is rule one: if clothes ever catch fire, you stop. You never, ever run.

"DROP!" Down went our hero onto the soft rug, hands covering his face, safe and snug. "Now ROLL!" called Cinder, cheering from beside the big safety checklist on the wall. Back and forth he rolled, giggling the whole way, until the practice was perfect: rolling back and forth is what puts the flames out.

Then the whole tiered crowd on the steps clapped, and everyone promised to practice at home — on a rug, with a grown-up, laughing allowed. Because a move you have practiced for fun is a move you remember for real.

The pros: "When the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke." — Champaign Fire Department, IL (Source: https://champaignil.gov/2022/10/05/champaign-fire-department-celebrates-the-100th-anniversary-of-fire-prevention-week/)
