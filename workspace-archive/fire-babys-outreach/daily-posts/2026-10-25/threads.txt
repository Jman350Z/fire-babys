🛑 STOP, DROP AND ROLL SUNDAY 🛑

If clothes catch fire: STOP where you are (never run), DROP to the ground, cover your face, ROLL back and forth until the flames are out.

Practice it on a rug today — calm practice builds the reflex.

Fire Babys teaches fire safety through play for everyone. Proceeds are intended to fund U.S. fire-department school programs — no partnerships yet.
