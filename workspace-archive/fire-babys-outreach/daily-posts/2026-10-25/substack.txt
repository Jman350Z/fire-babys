Stop, Drop and Roll Sunday — A Letter to Grown-Ups

Dear families,

Today's ten-minute task is a drill, not a lecture: teach everyone at home what to do if clothes ever catch fire. STOP right where you are — running feeds flames with air. DROP to the ground and cover your face with your hands. ROLL back and forth until the flames are completely out, then cool any burn with clean, cool water and get medical help.

Make it a game on a soft rug, the way the Fire Babys crew practices in today's concept art. Kids who have rolled for fun remember how to roll for real.

The pros: "When the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke." — Champaign Fire Department, IL (Source: https://champaignil.gov/2022/10/05/champaign-fire-department-celebrates-the-100th-anniversary-of-fire-prevention-week/)

Fire Babys games teach fire safety through play for everyone; proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day. No partnerships yet — that's the mission we're building toward.
