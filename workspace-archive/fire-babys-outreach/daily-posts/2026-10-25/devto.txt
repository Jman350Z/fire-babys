Stop, Drop and Roll: A Three-State Safety Mechanic

Good game design turns a rule into a reflex. Stop, Drop and Roll is exactly that: a three-state sequence a player can learn in one round and execute under pressure. STOP — freeze, because running feeds flames with air. DROP — get to the ground and cover your face. ROLL — roll back and forth until the flames are out.

That is why today's Fire Babys concept art stages it as a drill, not a disaster: Blaze calls the stop, our hero rolls happily on a living-room rug, and Cinder cheers from the safety checklist poster. No danger, no harm — just rehearsal, the way real safety training works.

The real-world spec comes from the pros: "When the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke." — Champaign Fire Department, IL (Source: https://champaignil.gov/2022/10/05/champaign-fire-department-celebrates-the-100th-anniversary-of-fire-prevention-week/)

Fire Babys is a game project teaching fire safety through play for everyone. Game proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day — no partnerships exist yet, and we say so plainly.
