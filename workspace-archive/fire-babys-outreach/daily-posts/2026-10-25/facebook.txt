🛑 STOP, DROP AND ROLL SUNDAY 🛑

Today's family mission is the three words every person should know by heart: STOP, DROP, and ROLL. If clothes ever catch fire: STOP right where you are — never run, running feeds the flames. DROP to the ground and cover your face with your hands. ROLL back and forth until the flames are out.

Practice it together on a soft rug today, just like the crew in today's concept art — Blaze calls STOP, our hero drops and rolls, and Cinder cheers every practice run. Practicing when it's calm is how the moves are there when it counts.

The pros: "When the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke." — Champaign Fire Department, IL (Source: https://champaignil.gov/2022/10/05/champaign-fire-department-celebrates-the-100th-anniversary-of-fire-prevention-week/)

Fire Babys games teach fire safety through play for everyone; proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day. No partnerships yet — that's the mission we're building toward.
