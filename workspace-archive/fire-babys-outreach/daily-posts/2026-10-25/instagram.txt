🛑 STOP, DROP AND ROLL SUNDAY 🛑

Three words that put out flames on clothes: STOP. DROP. ROLL.

✅ STOP — freeze right where you are. Never run.
✅ DROP — get down on the ground, cover your face with your hands.
✅ ROLL — roll back and forth until the flames are out. Then cool the burn with water and get help.

Practice on a rug today — calm practice builds real reflexes.

The pros: "When the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke." — Champaign Fire Department, IL
