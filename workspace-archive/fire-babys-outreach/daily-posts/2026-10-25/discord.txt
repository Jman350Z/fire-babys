🛑 **STOP, DROP AND ROLL SUNDAY** 🛑

Server challenge: can everyone in your house say the three moves without thinking? If clothes catch fire: STOP where you are — never run. DROP to the ground and cover your face. ROLL back and forth until the flames are out.

Blaze calls STOP, our hero drops and rolls on the rug, and Cinder cheers in today's concept art. Run your own practice round today — on a soft rug, laughing counts, the reflex is the prize.

The pros: "When the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke." — Champaign Fire Department, IL (Source: https://champaignil.gov/2022/10/05/champaign-fire-department-celebrates-the-100th-anniversary-of-fire-prevention-week/)
