🌳 MEETING PLACE SUNDAY! 🌳

It's Sunday, and the Fire Babys have a question for every family: if everyone had to get out fast, where would you all meet? One big tree, one mailbox, one neighbor's porch — just ONE spot everyone knows by heart.

Today's picture shows Blaze, Ruko, and Cinder at their tree with the big wooden sign: OUR MEETING PLACE. Ruko drew the escape map, Cinder holds the EXIT badge, and Blaze is pointing at the tree like "that's the one!"

Real talk from real firefighters: "Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things. Smoke is poisonous." — U.S. Fire Administration (USFA) (Source: https://www.safeoutcome.com/documents/00120/USFA%20Escape%20Planning.pdf)

Once everyone is at the meeting place, you can count heads and call 911 from outside — nobody ever goes back in. That's how the pros do it.
