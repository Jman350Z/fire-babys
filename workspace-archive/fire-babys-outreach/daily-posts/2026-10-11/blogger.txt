## The Great Meeting-Place Debate

Blaze, Ruko, and Cinder stood on the lawn with Ruko's brand-new escape map, and they had a serious problem. Not the kind with ladders or hoses. The kind with three fire babies and three very different opinions.

"The meeting place should be the big tree," declared Blaze, pointing at the tree with the wooden sign. "It's the tallest. You can see it from anywhere."

"The mailbox," said Cinder, holding the EXIT badge overhead. "It's right by the road. The firefighters would see us first."

Ruko tapped the map. "HOME. EXIT. MEET HERE. It just has to be ONE spot that everyone remembers." He drew a star exactly where the tree stood. "The tree wins. It's the one we'd never forget."

And so the crew marched to the big tree, Blaze leading the parade, Ruko reading the map upside-down (he swears it wasn't), and Cinder waving the EXIT badge like a tiny trophy. They stood under the sign — OUR MEETING PLACE — and practiced the rule the professionals live by.

As the firefighters at the U.S. Fire Administration teach: "Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things. Smoke is poisonous. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your exit." (Source: https://www.safeoutcome.com/documents/00120/USFA%20Escape%20Planning.pdf)

Your family's turn: hold your own meeting-place debate tonight. Tree, mailbox, porch — whatever wins, make sure every single person knows it by heart. That's the whole game.
