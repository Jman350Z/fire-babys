Dear Fire Babys Family,

It's Meeting Place Sunday, and I have a five-minute ask for the grown-ups. Tonight at dinner, ask the kids one question: "If the smoke alarm went off right now, where would we all meet?" If anyone hesitates — that's your sign. Pick one spot together: the big tree, the mailbox, the neighbor's porch. One place, memorized by everyone.

Then walk it once. Out both exits, straight to the spot, count heads, done. Kids learn by moving, not by lecture. Blaze, Ruko, and Cinder practiced on the lawn in today's picture — map in hand, meeting tree picked, smiles all around. Make it a game, not a scare.

The firefighters who teach this for a living say it best: "If the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your way out. Call 911 from outside your home." — South Beach Regional Fire Authority, WA

That one conversation, once, at dinner — and your family is measurably safer. That is the whole point of what we're building here: fire safety that kids actually remember because they played it out.

Fire Babys games teach fire safety through play. Game proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day — no partnerships exist yet; that is the mission we are working toward together.

With love,

Bobby Rabinowitz
