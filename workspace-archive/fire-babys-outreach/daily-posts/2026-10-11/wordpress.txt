# Sunday Home Check: Two Ways Out, One Meeting Place

Sundays are for resetting the week — so reset your family's fire plan while you're at it. This one takes ten minutes and needs no tools: just your feet and your eyes.

First, walk your home's exits. Every room needs TWO ways out — door and window both count. Open the windows; make sure they aren't painted shut. Clear the paths: move the boxes, the laundry basket, the toys away from the doors and windows you'd actually use. A blocked exit in a drill becomes a blocked exit in a real fire.

Second, pick your meeting place and walk to it. One tree, one mailbox, one porch — outside, a safe distance from the home. Stand there together and agree: when the alarm sounds, everyone comes HERE, and nobody leaves until every head is counted. Then call 911 from there. Never go back inside — not for people, not for pets, not for anything.

The National Fire Protection Association puts it without wiggle room: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home." (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)

Third, practice it once at walking pace, kids included. Blaze, Ruko, and Cinder ran theirs this morning on the lawn — map, EXIT badge, and all. If they can do it, so can you.
