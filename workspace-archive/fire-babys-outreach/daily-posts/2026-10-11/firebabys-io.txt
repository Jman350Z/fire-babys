# Meeting Place Sunday: Draw Your Family's Escape Map

Today's new Fire Babys art shows exactly what Sunday is for: Ruko unrolls a hand-drawn escape plan — HOME, EXIT, and a star marked MEET HERE. Your family can do the same thing in about ten minutes.

Grab a crayon and a sheet of paper. Draw your home's rough shape, then mark TWO ways out of every room — door and window both count. Pick one meeting place outside: a big tree, a mailbox, a neighbor's porch. Everyone memorizes it. When the alarm sounds, everyone goes straight there and stays there, so you can count heads and nobody wanders back inside.

The professionals put it plainly: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)

Sunday tip: walk the whole plan once, at a normal pace, kids included. Practice turns panic into habit. Blaze, Ruko, and Cinder already picked their tree — yours is waiting.
