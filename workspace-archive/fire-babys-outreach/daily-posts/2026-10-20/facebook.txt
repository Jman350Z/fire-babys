🚪 TWO WAYS OUT TUESDAY 🚪

Every room needs two ways out — a door and a window, or two doors. If one way is blocked by smoke or heat, the second way is how your family gets outside safely.

Today's family mission: walk room to room together and name both ways out loud in each one. Check that windows open, doors are not blocked, and everyone knows where to meet outside once you are out — Blaze, Ruko & Cinder picked the big tree out front.

The pros: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)
