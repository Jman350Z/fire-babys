Blaze and the Two Ways Out

Blaze stood in the living room and pointed at the front door. "Way Out One!" Then Ruko pulled back the curtain and grinned. "And the window is Way Out Two!" Cinder wrote both on the big board and drew the big tree outside — the meeting place.

That afternoon the whole squad walked from room to room, naming two ways out in each one, checking that no boxes blocked a door and that every window could open. When they finished, every Fire Baby could say both ways without looking at the board.

Try it in your home today: pick one room, name its two ways out, and walk to your meeting place together.

The pros: "Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things. Smoke is poisonous. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your exit." — U.S. Fire Administration (USFA) (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)
