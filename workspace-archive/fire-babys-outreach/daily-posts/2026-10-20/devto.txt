Game Design Note: Why "Two Ways Out" Is a Great Level Rule

Good level design always gives the player a second route. Real fire safety teaches the same principle: two ways out of every room. In Fire Babys, that becomes a design rule — every space the player explores has a primary exit and a backup exit, and learning to spot both is the skill the game rewards.

Today's concept art shows Blaze, Ruko & Cinder teaching that rule on an escape-plan board: door first, window second, meeting place outside. No gameplay footage — concept only, as always for our public posts.

The pros: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)

Fire Babys games teach fire safety through play for everyone; proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day. No partnerships yet — that's the mission.
