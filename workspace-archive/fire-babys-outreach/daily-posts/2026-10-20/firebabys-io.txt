Two Ways Out: The Rule That Saves Lives When One Exit Is Blocked

A fire can block a hallway in seconds. That is why safety pros teach two ways out of every room: usually a door and a window. If the first way is hot or smoky, you do not waste time — you use the second way, get low under smoke, and go straight to your outside meeting place.

Try it at home today: draw a simple floor plan, mark two exits per room, check that windows actually open, and practice feeling doors with the back of your hand — a hot door means use the other way out.

The pros: "Get out, stay out and stay alive! Crawl low under smoke — smoke rises, so cleaner, cooler air is near the floor. Get down on your hands and knees and crawl low under the smoke to the nearest exit." — Lynbrook Fire Department, NY (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)
