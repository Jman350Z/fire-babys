Two Ways Out Tuesday — A Letter to Grown-Ups

Charging Safety Week is complete — thank you to everyone who took the pledge with us. Today we return to the foundation underneath everything: two ways out of every room.

Please take ten minutes this week to walk your home with your family. Name the door and the window in each room, clear anything blocking them, confirm the windows open, and agree on one outside meeting place. It is a small walk that makes a real escape plan real.

The pros: "If the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your way out. Call 911 from outside your home." — South Beach Regional Fire Authority, WA (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)
