Dear Fire Babys Family,

Cinder here with a question for your dinner table tonight: if your family had to leave the house in a hurry, where would you all meet? Not the porch — not far enough. Pick one clear spot a safe distance away: the big oak, the neighbor's fence, the corner mailbox. Write it down. Tell the babysitter. Tell grandma. Everyone who walks through your door should know the spot.

Here's why it matters: in a real fire, the scariest moment isn't getting out — it's standing outside wondering if everyone else made it. A meeting place turns that fear into a headcount. One look, everyone accounted for, zero guessing.

The NFPA says it best: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)

Fire Babys games are built to teach kids fire safety through play, and proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day. No partnerships exist yet — that's the dream we're working toward.
