The Night We Drew Our Way Out

Last night, the kids and I spread a big sheet of paper on the kitchen table and drew our house — every room, every door, every window. Then we drew the important part: two ways out of each room, in bright red crayon. The kids argued over which window was "theirs." Then we picked our meeting spot: the big tree by the sidewalk. It's on the fridge now, right next to tonight's Fire Babys art.

Here's what surprised me: my youngest asked, "What if the hallway is full of smoke?" — and we got to practice the answer together. Get low. The clean air is near the floor. Feel the door first; if it's hot, take the second way out.

The NFPA's words are worth memorizing as a family: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)

Fire Babys games are built to teach kids fire safety through play, and proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day. No partnerships exist yet — that's the dream we're working toward.
