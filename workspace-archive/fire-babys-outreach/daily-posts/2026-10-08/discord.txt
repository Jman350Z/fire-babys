@everyone 🔥 FAMILY FIRE DRILL NIGHT — THIS WEEKEND 🔥

New challenge from Boulder the Gentle Wall: run a real fire drill at home this weekend. Pick a surprise moment, hit a timer, and see how fast your whole crew gets outside to the meeting spot. Under 2 minutes = LEGEND status. Post your family's time in #fire-drills — no capes required.

Drill rules: everyone goes low under "smoke" (a blanket held up works), feel doors before opening, and once you're out, you STAY out. The U.S. Fire Administration says: "Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things. Smoke is poisonous. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your exit." (Source: https://www.safeoutcome.com/documents/00120/USFA%20Escape%20Planning.pdf)

Fire Babys games are built to teach kids fire safety through play, and proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day. No partnerships exist yet — that's the dream we're working toward.
