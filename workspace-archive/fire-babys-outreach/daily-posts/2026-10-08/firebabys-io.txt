Two Ways Out: Tonight's 15-Minute Family Mission

Tonight's Fire Babys art says it all: Blaze and Ruko are pointing at the family fire-escape map on the fridge — because every home needs one. Grab a piece of paper, sit down with your kids, and draw your home together. Mark two ways out of every room: a door and a window, a front hall and a back stair. Then pick your meeting tree — one spot outside, like the big tree or the mailbox, where everyone gathers.

Now walk it. Take the kids through both routes, for real, with the lights on first and then again low to the floor. Firefighters drill this because in a real fire you may have only minutes — practice turns panic into a plan your body already knows.

Here's the rule the U.S. Fire Administration wants every family to memorize: "Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things. Smoke is poisonous. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your exit." (Source: https://www.safeoutcome.com/documents/00120/USFA%20Escape%20Planning.pdf)
