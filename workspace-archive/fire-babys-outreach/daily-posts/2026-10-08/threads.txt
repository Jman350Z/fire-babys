🧵 The #1 way house fires start? Cooking. Left alone for "just a minute."

Ruko's kitchen rules: stay in the kitchen while the stove is on, keep towels and sleeves away from the flame, and if a pan catches fire — slide a lid over it and turn off the heat. Never carry a burning pan. Never use water on a grease fire.

The U.S. Fire Administration keeps it simple: "Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things. Smoke is poisonous. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your exit." (Source: https://www.safeoutcome.com/documents/00120/USFA%20Escape%20Planning.pdf)
