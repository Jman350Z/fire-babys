🔥 BEEP TEST THURSDAY 🔥

Fire Babys family — real talk: when's the last time you pressed the test button on your smoke alarm? Today's challenge takes 30 seconds. Press it, hear the beep, and make sure every kid in your home knows exactly what that sound means. A working alarm is the loudest hero in your house.

Blaze's rule of thumb: if your alarm is over 10 years old, it's earned its retirement — swap in a fresh one. And tonight at dinner, ask your kids one question: "If the alarm went off right now, what's our plan?" You'll be glad you asked.

The pros put it plainly: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)
