🔋 CHARGING SAFETY WEEK — DAY 3 (Discord)

Day 3: STAY NEAR WHILE IT CHARGES.

The rules: charge devices where a grown-up can see them, stay in the same area while they charge, and never leave a charging device alone in an empty room or charging overnight where nobody can check it. A device that feels hot, smells strange, or looks swollen gets unplugged if it's safe — and a grown-up is told right away.

Today's server challenge: help your family pick ONE charging spot at home — hard, flat, out in the open. Found yours? Post a 🔋 when it's set up with a grown-up — never share personal info or addresses.

The pros: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)
