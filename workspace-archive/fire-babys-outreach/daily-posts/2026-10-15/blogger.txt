Blaze and the Charging Spot — Charging Safety Week, Day 3

Blaze wanted to charge the tablet in a secret fort. Ruko shook his head. "If nobody can see it, nobody can help it," he said.

So Cinder helped them carry everything to the kitchen counter instead — hard, flat, and right where a grown-up could see. They stayed nearby, played a card game, and unplugged the tablet as soon as it was full.

The fort stayed secret. The charging didn't. That was the deal, and everybody liked it.

The pros: "When the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke." — Champaign Fire Department, IL (Source: https://champaignil.gov/2022/10/05/champaign-fire-department-celebrates-the-100th-anniversary-of-fire-prevention-week/)
