Charging Safety Week, Day 3 — The Stay-Nearby Rule

A charging device should never be a secret. Day 3 of Charging Safety Week is about location and attention: charge on a hard, flat surface where a grown-up can see it, stay nearby while it charges, and don't leave devices charging alone for hours or overnight.

Why it matters is simple. Most charging problems start small — heat, a strange smell, a swelling battery. Somebody nearby notices those early signs. Nobody notices them in an empty room.

Pick one family charging spot today and make it the house rule.

The pros: "Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things. Smoke is poisonous. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your exit." — U.S. Fire Administration (USFA) (Source: https://www.safeoutcome.com/documents/00120/USFA%20Escape%20Planning.pdf)
