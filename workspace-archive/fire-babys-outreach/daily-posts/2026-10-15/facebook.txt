🔋 CHARGING SAFETY WEEK — DAY 3 🔋

Day 3 mission for families: charge where a grown-up can see it, stay nearby, and never leave a charging device all alone.

Blaze, Ruko & Cinder set up today's charging spot on the kitchen counter — out in the open, on a hard surface, with a grown-up close by. No charging hidden under pillows, no devices left charging alone in an empty room, and no overnight charging where nobody can see it.

Try it at home today: pick ONE family charging spot together. It should be on a hard, flat surface, in a room where a grown-up spends time, and easy to check. If a device feels hot, smells strange, or looks swollen, stop, unplug it if it's safe, and tell a grown-up right away.

The pros: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)
