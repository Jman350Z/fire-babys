Charging Safety Week, Day 3 — The Stay-Nearby Checklist

Use this Day 3 checklist with a grown-up:

1. Pick one charging spot: hard, flat, out in the open.
2. Charge where a grown-up can see the device.
3. Stay nearby while it charges — no leaving it alone in an empty room.
4. Don't charge overnight where nobody can check it.
5. Hot, strange-smelling, or swollen? Stop, unplug if safe, and tell a grown-up.
6. Unplug when it's full.

Small habits, checked daily, are what Charging Safety Week is for.
