Charging Safety Week, Day 3 — A Letter to Grown-Ups: Stay Near While It Charges

Dear Fan Club,

Day 3 is about where charging happens — and who is around when it does.

A phone charging alone in a bedroom, a tablet charging overnight under a blanket fort, an e-bike battery charging in a closed garage: the device is out of sight, so the early warning signs are out of sight too. Heat, a strange smell, or a swelling battery only help if somebody is there to notice.

Today's Fire Babys mission is to set up one charging spot: hard, flat, out in the open, in a room where adults actually spend time. Then stay nearby while things charge, and unplug when they're done.

The pros: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)

Fire Babys games teach fire safety through play; proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day. No partnerships yet — that's the mission we're building toward.
