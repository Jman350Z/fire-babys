Designing exits kids remember: level design as a safety lesson

Every Fire Babys level is built on one quiet rule: there are always two ways out. Doors, windows, a second stairwell — players learn to scan for a backup exit the way real firefighters teach families to. It's never written on a loading screen; it's baked into the map, so kids absorb it by playing.

We also design our "respawn" thinking around real life. When our hero goes down, he returns at the castle — the team's safe gathering point. That's the meeting-place rule wearing a game costume: when things go wrong, everybody regroups at the one spot the whole family already knows.

The Plainfield Fire Protection District teaches the matching real-world skill: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)

Fire Babys proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day. No partnerships exist yet — we're building the games, and the safety curriculum inside them, first.
