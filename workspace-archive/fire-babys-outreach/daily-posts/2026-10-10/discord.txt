🔥 WEEKEND CHALLENGE: THE 2-MINUTE DRILL 🔥

Fire Babys crew, it's Drill Day! This weekend's community challenge: run ONE practice escape with your family and time it. Out the door, straight to your meeting place, everybody together — then beat your own time on round two.

Share your drill time (just the time — never your address!) and tell us your meeting-place style: tree 🌳, mailbox 📬, or neighbor's porch 🏡. Blaze is on stopwatch duty and Ruko checks the map, so your family gets the full Drill Day treatment too.

The Lynbrook Fire Department's rally cry: "Get out, stay out and stay alive!" — Lynbrook Fire Department, NY (Source: http://lynbrookfd.org/beta05/fire-prevention.html)
