🚪 CLOSE BEFORE YOU DOZE 🚪

Quick Saturday habit with a superpower: sleep with bedroom doors closed. A closed door slows smoke and heat, buying precious time — and it costs zero dollars and ten seconds a night.

Make it a game tonight: every Fire Baby "closes before they doze." Doors shut, hallway clear, alarm tested this month. Small habits, big protection.

Plainfield Fire Protection District teaches: "Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)

Fire Babys games teach fire safety through play; proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day. No partnerships yet — that's the mission.
