The Great Backyard Drill Day

Saturday morning at the Fire Babys' house started with a banner over the porch: FIRE DRILL DAY! Blaze polished his stopwatch until it shone. "Today," he announced, "we practice being the fastest safe family on the block!" Ruko unrolled the escape map — kitchen, bedroom, front door, and a big gold star on the meetup spot under the tree.

"Alarm check first!" said Cinder, holding up the round white smoke alarm with both hands. Beep-beep-beep! Everybody covered their ears and giggled. It wasn't scary. It was just the sound that means: time to move, team. Then out they went — through the kitchen door, down the path, and straight to the tree with the little wooden sign: SAFETY MEETUP, THIS WAY.

Blaze clicked the stopwatch and cheered. They'd made it, all together, with seconds to spare — so they did it again using the second way out, high-fiving at the tree. That's the Fire Babys secret: practice is just a game you play until your whole family knows it by heart. As Lynbrook Fire Department says: "Crawl low under smoke — smoke rises, so cleaner, cooler air is near the floor." — Lynbrook Fire Department, NY (Source: http://lynbrookfd.org/beta05/fire-prevention.html) Fire Babys stories teach fire safety through play, and game proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day — no partnerships exist yet.
