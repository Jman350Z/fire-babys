Saturday Home Check: Doors, Windows, and Clear Ways Out

A fire escape plan only works if the exits actually work. This Saturday, give your routes a ten-minute readiness check: every door on your escape path should open smoothly, windows should unstick and open with a grown-up's help, and hallways and stairs should be clear of shoes, toys, and storage boxes that love to migrate into walkways.

Make it a family job. Kids can be the "path inspectors," pointing out anything blocking a door or crowding a hallway, while grown-ups handle locks, latches, and anything heavy. Finish by walking the full route together — out the door and straight to your outside meeting place, the same ending as every Fire Babys drill.

And if the alarm ever does sound for real, the Champaign Fire Department's rule closes the loop: "When the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke." — Champaign Fire Department, IL (Source: https://champaignil.gov/2022/10/05/champaign-fire-department-celebrates-the-100th-anniversary-of-fire-prevention-week/)
