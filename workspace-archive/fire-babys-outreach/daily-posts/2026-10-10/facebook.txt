🔥 SATURDAY DRILL DAY: BEAT THE CLOCK 🔥

Happy Saturday, families! Today's Fire Babys mission is a friendly race: run your home fire drill and time it. Blaze has the stopwatch, Ruko has the escape map, and the goal is everyone out the door and together at your meeting place — calm, quick, and as a team. Grown-ups set the pace; kids stick with their grown-up the whole way.

Try it twice today: once walking the main way out, and once using a second way out of a bedroom. If a door or window is hard to open, that's not a fail — that's a fix-it clue for this week. Practice now is what turns a scary surprise into a plan your family already knows.

As the U.S. Fire Administration puts it: "Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things." — U.S. Fire Administration (USFA) (Source: https://www.safeoutcome.com/documents/00120/USFA%20Escape%20Planning.pdf)

Fire Babys games teach kids fire safety through play, and proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day. No partnerships exist yet — that's the mission we're building toward.
