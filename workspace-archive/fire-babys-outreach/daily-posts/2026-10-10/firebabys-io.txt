Fire Drill Day: Clear the Way Out

Look at today's art: Blaze is timing the drill, Ruko is holding the family's escape map, and Cinder is carrying the smoke alarm — all of them gathered on the front lawn by the "Safety Meetup" tree. That happy ending only works if the way out is clear, so today's mission is a path check: walk every exit route in your home and move anything blocking it. Shoes, toys, and boxes love to pile up by doors; today they find a new home.

While you walk, test that doors and windows on the route open easily — with a grown-up's help — and that hallways stay clear enough to move fast, even in the dark. Clear paths, working exits, one meeting place outside: that's the whole checklist, and most families can do it in about ten minutes.

Here's the rule the National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) wants every family to remember: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)
