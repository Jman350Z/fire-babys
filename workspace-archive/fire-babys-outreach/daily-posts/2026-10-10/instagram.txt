🔥 PRESS THE BUTTON SATURDAY 🔥

That little button on your smoke alarm is a superpower — use it! Once a month, a grown-up presses the test button so kids can hear the beep-beep-beep while everything is calm and safe. When children know the sound ahead of time, it feels like a signal they've practiced, not a scary surprise.

Cinder is showing off our alarm today for a reason: a working alarm is the first step of every escape plan. Test yours this Saturday, check the date on the back together, and let your kids be the official "button day" helpers with a grown-up right beside them.

The Champaign Fire Department says it simply: "When the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out." — Champaign Fire Department, IL (Source: https://champaignil.gov/2022/10/05/champaign-fire-department-celebrates-the-100th-anniversary-of-fire-prevention-week/)
