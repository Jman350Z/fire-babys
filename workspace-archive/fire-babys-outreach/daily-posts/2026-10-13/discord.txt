🔋 CHARGING SAFETY WEEK — DAY 1 (Discord kickoff)

All week we're posting one charging-safety theme a day. Day 1: HARD, FLAT SURFACE.

The rule: phones, tablets, and game controllers charge on a table or counter — never on a bed, couch, blanket, or under a pillow. Soft surfaces trap heat while a device charges.

Today's server challenge: check your own charging spot. Hard and flat? Post a 🔋 when it's fixed — table/counter only, never share personal info or addresses.

The pros: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)
