Blaze and the Charging Table — a Charging Safety Week story

On the first morning of Charging Safety Week, Ruko carried his tablet to the couch, ready to plug it in between the cushions. "Stop right there!" said Blaze, wheeling in a little wooden table. "Chargers don't nap on couches. They work on tables!"

Cinder read the checklist out loud: "Hard, flat surface — check! Nothing covering the tablet — check! Space all around — check!" The tablet charged happily on the table while the three friends played nearby, and Ruko grinned: "Soft is for sitting. Hard and flat is for charging."

The pros: "Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things. Smoke is poisonous. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your exit." — U.S. Fire Administration (USFA) (Source: https://www.safeoutcome.com/documents/00120/USFA%20Escape%20Planning.pdf)
