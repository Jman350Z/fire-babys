Charging Safety Week, Day 1: A letter to grown-ups about where the phones sleep

Dear Fan Club families,

This week is Charging Safety Week at Fire Babys, and Day 1 starts with a question: where do the devices in your house charge overnight? If the answer is a nightstand piled with bedding, a couch cushion, or under a pillow, today's habit is for you — move charging to a hard, flat surface like a kitchen counter or a clear table.

Lithium batteries charge best, and safest, with room to breathe. Hard and flat lets heat escape; soft and covered traps it. It's a ten-second move that becomes a lifetime habit for kids who watch you do it.

The pros: "Get out, stay out and stay alive! Crawl low under smoke — smoke rises, so cleaner, cooler air is near the floor. Get down on your hands and knees and crawl low under the smoke to the nearest exit." — Lynbrook Fire Department, NY (Source: http://lynbrookfd.org/beta05/fire-prevention.html)
