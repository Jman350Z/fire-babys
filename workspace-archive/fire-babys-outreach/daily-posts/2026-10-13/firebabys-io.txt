Charging Safety Week — Day 1: The Hard, Flat Surface Rule

Welcome to Charging Safety Week! All week the Fire Babys are sharing one simple charging habit a day. Day 1 is the golden rule: always charge phones, tablets, and other lithium-battery devices on a hard, flat surface — a table or counter — never on a bed, couch, or under a pillow.

Soft surfaces trap heat. A hard, flat surface lets air move and heat escape, and keeping the area around the device clear makes charging safer still. Today's concept art shows Blaze, Ruko, and Cinder running their charging station exactly that way, checklist and all.

The pros: "Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things. Smoke is poisonous. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your exit." — U.S. Fire Administration (USFA) (Source: https://www.safeoutcome.com/documents/00120/USFA%20Escape%20Planning.pdf)
