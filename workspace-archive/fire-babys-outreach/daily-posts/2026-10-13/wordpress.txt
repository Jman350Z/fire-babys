Charging Safety Week Checklist — Day 1: Set Up a Safe Charging Spot

Charging Safety Week runs October 13–19, and Day 1 is setup day. Pick ONE charging spot for the whole family and make it pass this checklist: hard, flat surface (table or counter); nothing covering devices while they charge; clear space around every device; chargers where grown-ups can see them.

Why it matters: soft surfaces like beds and couches trap the heat a battery makes while charging. A hard, flat, clear spot lets that heat escape — the simplest charging habit a family can build.

The pros: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)
