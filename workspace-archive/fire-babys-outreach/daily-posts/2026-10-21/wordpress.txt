🔔 SMOKE ALARM WEDNESDAY — the full checklist 🔔

Blaze, Ruko & Cinder's alarm checklist, checked off in today's concept art:

✅ One smoke alarm inside every bedroom
✅ One outside each sleeping area
✅ One on every level of the home
✅ Test every alarm once a month — press the button, hear the beep
✅ Chirping that won't stop? A grown-up changes the battery today
✅ Alarm older than 10 years? A grown-up replaces the whole alarm
✅ Everyone in the home knows the sound — and knows it means: get out, stay out, meet at the outside meeting place, call 911 from outside

The pros: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)
