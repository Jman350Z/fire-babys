🔔 SMOKE ALARM WEDNESDAY — a design note from the Fire Babys crew 🔔

In our games, the smoke alarm is the level's first NPC: it never fights, never scores — it warns. That's deliberate. Real alarms buy the one resource no power-up can: time. So today's concept art puts Blaze on the step stool pressing the test button while Ruko works the checklist — test monthly, change the battery, plan & practice.

The design rule we stole from real firefighters: a warning only works if everyone recognizes it instantly. Test day teaches the sound in calm conditions, so the real moment needs no tutorial.

The pros: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)

Fire Babys games teach fire safety through play for everyone; proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day. No partnerships yet — that's the mission.
