Blaze and the Two Beeps

Blaze counted the alarms in the clubhouse and found one missing from the hallway. "An alarm you can't hear can't warn you," Ruko said, checking his clipboard. So the crew placed one in every bedroom, one outside the sleeping area, and one on every level — then pressed every test button, one by one, until the whole house sang BEEP, BEEP, BEEP.

Cinder wrote the rule on the board: test them every month, and when the beep sounds for real, everybody goes out the door, across the yard, to the big tree — and nobody goes back in.

The pros: "Get out, stay out and stay alive! Crawl low under smoke — smoke rises, so cleaner, cooler air is near the floor. Get down on your hands and knees and crawl low under the smoke to the nearest exit." — Lynbrook Fire Department, NY (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)
