🔔 SMOKE ALARM WEDNESDAY 🔔

Family mission today: walk your home with a grown-up and find every smoke alarm. There should be one inside every bedroom, one outside each sleeping area, and one on every level of the home. Press the test button together — that loud beep means it's awake and watching.

Blaze, Ruko & Cinder's rule: test alarms once a month, and if an alarm is more than 10 years old, it's time for a grown-up to replace the whole alarm. A working alarm is the earliest warning a family ever gets.

The pros: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)
