🔔 SMOKE ALARM WEDNESDAY 🔔

Dear Fan Club grown-ups,

Today's ten-minute home walk is the simplest one we know: find your smoke alarms. One inside every bedroom, one outside each sleeping area, one on every level. Press each test button and listen for the beep. No beep — or a weak chirp that won't stop — means a grown-up changes the battery today, and an alarm older than 10 years gets replaced entirely.

Then let the little ones hear the real sound once, calmly, so it never surprises them at night. When it sounds for real, the rule never changes: out, stay out, call 911 from outside.

The pros: "If the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your way out. Call 911 from outside your home." — South Beach Regional Fire Authority, WA (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)
