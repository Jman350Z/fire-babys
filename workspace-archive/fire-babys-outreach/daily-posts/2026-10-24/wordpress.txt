Two Ways Out Saturday — The Full Family Checklist

Every room, two ways out. Use this checklist today: 1) In each room, name the door and the second way (window or another door). 2) Make sure windows open easily and everyone knows how a grown-up helps. 3) Feel doors before opening — hot means closed, use way two. 4) Draw the floor plan with both arrows. 5) Walk both routes to your one outside meeting place.

Practice in daylight and once in the evening, so the routes are familiar in any conditions. Once you're out, stay out — no going back for belongings or pets — and call 911 from outside.

The pros: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.pprfd.com/fire-safety-planning.html)
