🚪 TWO WAYS OUT SATURDAY 🚪

Every room needs TWO ways out — usually a door and a window. If the first way is blocked by heat or smoke, you don't have to stop and think: you already know your second way.

Today's family mission: walk room to room together, name both ways out of each one, and draw your own floor-plan map like Blaze, Ruko & Cinder did in today's art. Practice opening the window, and agree on your outside meeting place.

The pros: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.pprfd.com/fire-safety-planning.html)
