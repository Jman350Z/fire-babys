🚪 TWO WAYS OUT SATURDAY 🚪

Door = way 1. Window = way 2. Every room, every time. 👇

✅ Walk each room with a grown-up
✅ Name BOTH ways out out loud
✅ Feel doors — hot door = use way 2
✅ Draw your floor-plan map
✅ Meet outside, stay outside, call 911 from outside

The pros: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.pprfd.com/fire-safety-planning.html)
