Two Ways Out Saturday — Blaze and the Two Doors

Blaze loved his bedroom door. It was blue, it creaked in a friendly way, and he used it every single day. "That's my way out!" he said.

"And what's your second way?" asked Cinder, pointing at the window. Blaze blinked. He had never thought about it. So the Fire Babys drew a map of the whole house — two arrows out of every room — and walked both ways until Blaze could do it with his eyes on the map and a smile on his face. Door first. Window if the door is hot. Meet at the tree. Stay out.

The pros: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.pprfd.com/fire-safety-planning.html)
