Two Ways Out Saturday — Level Design: The Backup Exit

Good level design never traps the player with one door. Real homes shouldn't either. Two ways out of every room is redundancy you can teach: a primary route (the door) and a backup route (a window or second door) that everyone has walked before they ever need it.

That's the design lesson behind today's Fire Babys concept art: Blaze, Ruko & Cinder don't just point at exits — they draw the floor plan, mark both arrows, and practice both routes to the same meeting place outside.

The pros: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.pprfd.com/fire-safety-planning.html)

Fire Babys games teach fire safety through play for everyone; proceeds are intended to fund real U.S. fire-department school programs one day. No partnerships yet — that's the mission we're building toward.
