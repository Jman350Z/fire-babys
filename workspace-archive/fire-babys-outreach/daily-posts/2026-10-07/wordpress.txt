Feel the door. Take the second way out.

Before opening any closed door in a fire, feel it with the back of your hand. A hot door means fire on the other side — don't open it. Use your second way out, get low under the smoke, and once you're outside, stay outside. Firefighters put it plainly: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/)

Practice the door-feel with your kids tonight — no fire needed, just a hallway and a plan. The Fire Babys drill in the sunny meadow; your living room works just as well.
