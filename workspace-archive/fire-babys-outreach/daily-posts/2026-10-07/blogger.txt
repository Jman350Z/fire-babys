A 10-minute family drill that could save your home

You don't need a weekend to get fire-ready — you need ten minutes and a plan. Tonight: 1) Test every smoke alarm. 2) Pick your outside meeting spot. 3) Walk two ways out of each bedroom with your kids.

Then say the rule out loud together: when the alarm sounds, we get out and we stay out. Firefighters say it best: "If the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your way out. Call 911 from outside your home." — South Beach Regional Fire Authority, WA (https://www.sbrfa.org/contact-us)

The Fire Babys cheer for every family that drills together — prepared kids are brave kids.
