This week's mission: pick your family's OUTSIDE MEETING SPOT.

Every family needs one place — a tree, a mailbox, a neighbor's porch — where everyone gathers after getting out. Draw it, name it, and make sure every kid in the house can point to it. Post your meeting spot name when it's picked!

Why it matters: firefighters need you OUT and accounted for. "When the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke." — Champaign Fire Department, IL (https://champaignil.gov/department/fire-department/)
