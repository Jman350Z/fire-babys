Stop, Drop, and Roll — starring Blaze, Ruko, and Cinder

Today's brand-new Fire Babys picture shows Blaze the Star-Crowned, Ruko the Hothead, and Cinder the Chaos Gremlin demonstrating the most famous fire-safety move of all: stop, drop, and roll. If clothes ever catch fire, you STOP where you are, DROP to the ground, and ROLL back and forth to smother the flames. Practice it on the grass like the Fire Babys — make it a game, not a scare.

The Fire Babys want every kid to know two more moves: feel doors before opening them — if a door is hot, use your second way out — and once you're outside, stay outside. Firefighters put it plainly: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/)

Our mission: Fire Babys exists to teach kids to prevent house fires and what to do when one is near. Game proceeds are intended to fund real US fire-department school programs — so the lessons you see here reach classrooms for free.
