When did you last test your smoke alarms? 👀

Smoke alarms are the heroes that never sleep — but they need a quick check once a month. Press the test button, listen for the beep, and swap batteries when they chirp. The Fire Babys do a monthly alarm check in the Fire Kingdom, and Blaze says your home deserves the same.

When that alarm sounds, the rule is simple: "If the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your way out. Call 911 from outside your home." — South Beach Regional Fire Authority, WA (https://www.sbrfa.org/contact-us)

Test yours today. It takes ten seconds.
