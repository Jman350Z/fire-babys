Fire safety in one move: GET LOW AND GO.

Smoke rises — so the cleanest air in a fire is near the floor. Drop to your hands and knees and crawl low under the smoke to the nearest exit. Teach it to a kid today; it takes 30 seconds and it saves lives.

"Get out, stay out and stay alive! Crawl low under smoke — smoke rises, so cleaner, cooler air is near the floor. Get down on your hands and knees and crawl low under the smoke to the nearest exit." — Lynbrook Fire Department, NY (http://www.lynbrookfd.org/)
