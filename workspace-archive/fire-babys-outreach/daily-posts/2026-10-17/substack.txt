🔋 CHARGING SAFETY WEEK — DAY 5: A Letter to Grown-Ups

Dear Fan Club grown-ups,

Day 5 is about the big batteries: e-bikes, scooters, and power tools. These charge outside whenever possible, away from doors and exit paths — never in a hallway, stairwell, or bedroom where a problem could block the way out.

Tonight's two-minute check: walk your exit paths. Is anything with a big battery charging on one of them? Move the charging spot outside or to an open area clear of exits, and let the kids in your life learn the rule by seeing it: big batteries, outside, grown-ups only.

The pros: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)
