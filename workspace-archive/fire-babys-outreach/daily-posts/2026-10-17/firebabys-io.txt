🔋 CHARGING SAFETY WEEK — DAY 5: The Big Battery Rule

The bigger the battery, the bigger the rule. E-bikes, electric scooters, and power tools should charge outside whenever possible, away from doors, windows used as exits, and escape paths — and only a grown-up plugs them in or moves them.

Why outside and away from exits? If a big battery ever overheats, nobody's way out should be blocked. Kids never handle big-battery charging: if a battery or device ever feels very hot, looks swollen, smells strange, or smokes, leave it alone, tell a grown-up right away, and get outside.

The pros: "Get out fast and stay out. Never go back inside for people, pets or things. Smoke is poisonous. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke to your exit." — U.S. Fire Administration (USFA) (Source: https://www.safeoutcome.com/documents/00120/USFA%20Escape%20Planning.pdf)
