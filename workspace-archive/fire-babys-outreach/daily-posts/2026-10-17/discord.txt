Day 5: BIG BATTERIES CHARGE OUTSIDE.

E-bikes, scooters, and power tools charge outside, away from doors and exits — never where they could block the way out. Grown-ups handle big batteries; kids know the rule and spot the safe spot.

Today's server challenge: with a grown-up, find where your family's biggest battery charges. Outside and clear of exits? Post a 🔋 — never share personal info or addresses.

The pros: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)
