Day 5: BIG BATTERIES CHARGE OUTSIDE.

E-bikes, scooters, and power tools carry big batteries — and big batteries get a grown-up rule: they charge outside, away from doors and exits, never blocking the way out. Blaze, Ruko & Cinder set up today's outdoor charging zone on the driveway, with the front door completely clear.

Today's family mission: with a grown-up, check where the big batteries in your home charge. If one charges by a doorway or in a hallway, pick a safer outdoor spot together.

The pros: "If the smoke alarm sounds, GET OUT AND STAY OUT. Never go back inside for people or pets. If you have to escape through smoke, GET LOW AND GO under the smoke to your way out. Call the fire department (911) from outside your home." — National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) (Source: https://www.longisland.com/news/01-06-17/nfpa-cpsc-fire-safety-challenge-toolkit.html)
