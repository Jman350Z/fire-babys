🔋 CHARGING SAFETY WEEK — DAY 5: "Blaze and the Big Battery"

The e-bike stood by the front door, plugged in and right in everyone's way. "Big batteries don't charge by exits," Blaze said. "If anything ever went wrong, that's our way out."

So with a grown-up doing all the plugging and moving, the Fire Babys set up a charging zone outside on the driveway — open air, clear space, door completely free. Ruko checked the path twice. "Out is always open now," he said.

The pros: "When the smoke alarm sounds, get out and stay out. If you must escape through smoke, get low and go under the smoke." — Champaign Fire Department, IL (Source: https://champaignil.gov/2022/10/05/champaign-fire-department-celebrates-the-100th-anniversary-of-fire-prevention-week/)
