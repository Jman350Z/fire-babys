🔋 CHARGING SAFETY WEEK — DAY 5: The Big Battery Checklist

Day 5 of Charging Safety Week covers the biggest batteries in the house.

✅ E-bikes, scooters & power tools charge outside when possible
✅ Never charge a big battery by a door, hallway, or exit path
✅ Only grown-ups plug in, unplug, or move big batteries
✅ Keep the charging spot clear, dry, and open
✅ Very hot, swollen, strange-smelling, or smoking? Don't touch it — tell a grown-up and get outside

The pros: "In a fire: get low and go. Feel doors before opening — if a door is hot, use your second way out. Once you're out, stay out. Never go back inside for belongings or pets. Call 911 from outside." — Plainfield Fire Protection District, IL (Source: https://www.plainfieldfpd.org/fire-prevention-resources)
