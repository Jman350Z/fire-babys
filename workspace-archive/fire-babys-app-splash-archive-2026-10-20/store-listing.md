## Full Description

🔥 MEET THE FIRE BABYS 🔥

The cutest little fire babies are on the run — and it's YOUR job to catch them!

In SPLASH, you play as a Fire Baby trying to stay DRY. Run from the chasers, dodge, weave, and don't get grabbed! Or flip it — grab your friends, carry them across the arena, and DUNK them in the pool with a satisfying POOF of steam!

**HOW TO PLAY:**
🏃 Run — you're the last one dry, stay that way!
🔥 Grab — catch a Fire Baby and carry them
💧 DUNK — toss them in the pool for the steam POOF!

**FEATURES:**
- Fast, hilarious multiplayer matches
- Two teams, one pool, zero mercy
- Adorable Fire Baby characters for everyone
- Quick 5-minute matches — perfect on the go
- Safe for everyone: no chat, no ads, no purchases

**FIRE SAFETY NOTE:** Fire Babys teaches fire and water safety through play — for everyone. In the real world, water beats fire — and these babies know it!
