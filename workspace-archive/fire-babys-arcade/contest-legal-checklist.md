# $10K Creator Competition — Legal Readiness Checklist (DRAFT for a lawyer)

**Status:** The public competition rules (COMPETITION-RULES.md) currently say
*"These rules are pending professional legal review before large-scale promotion."*
This checklist is the minimum a lawyer would need to clear. This is NOT legal
advice — it is a gap list so the review (or a free legal clinic) is fast.

## What the rules are missing (from the 2026-10-05 safety audit)

1. **End date / judging cadence** — rules say the competition "runs until the
   Fire Babys team announces a winner." An open-ended $10,000 promise is the
   biggest risk. Needs a fixed end date or a clear judging cadence.
2. **Tax language** — the $10,000 prize has tax consequences for the winner
   (1099 territory). Rules say nothing about who handles/withholds/reports.
3. **Governing law** — no state/country law named; no dispute-resolution venue.
4. **Void-where-prohibited** — no eligibility geography; some jurisdictions
   ban/restrict this kind of contest.
5. **Selection & tie criteria** — "from community favorites" is not a criterion.
   Needs: who judges, how votes are counted/weighted, how ties break.
6. **"The team's call is final" + "may end or extend at any time"** — combined
   with a $10,000 promise, the audit flagged this as reading like an illusory
   offer. A lawyer should reword or remove.
7. **Contest privacy notice for the Google Form** — the form collects real full
   name + phone number + mailing address (including minors via parent/guardian).
   The site privacy policy (privacy.html) covers game data only, not contest
   submissions. Needs: what PII is collected, why, how long it's kept, how to
   request deletion, and a link from the form itself.
