## THE 6 PILLARS OF POWER (our constitution)

1. **Synthesized Web Audio** — all sound generated in code. No audio files.
2. **Toyetic look, zero exceptions** — plushie-style flame babies. NEVER beaks, wings, feathers, realistic humans, or anything resembling Angry Birds.
3. **Silent slapstick shorts** — 15–30 second vertical videos, no dialogue. The marketing engine.
4. **NFPA matchup engine** — fire classes A/B/C/D vs. right extinguisher types. Real education in the game.
5. **Lean packaging** — frozen baselines, small files. Every kilobyte justifies itself.
6. **Fire Safety Academy** — USFA/NFPA curriculum in game + content. If it doesn't teach safety, it doesn't ship.
