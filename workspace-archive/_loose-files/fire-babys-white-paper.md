## 1. WHAT FIRE BABYS IS

Fire Babys is a children's battle game with a real mission: **teach kids to prevent house fires and what to do when a fire is nearby.**

It's not just a game — it's a fire-safety movement wrapped in characters kids love. Every match, every short video, every post serves the mission first and the game second.

- **Home base:** https://firebabys.io
- **Game launcher sites:** itch.io (live: https://firebabys.itch.io) and Game Jolt (account created, launching soon) — this is how we reach players and playtesters beyond our own site.
- **Community:** Discord (Fire Babys Official), Facebook group, Instagram, Threads, Substack Fan Club newsletter.

The name "Fire Babys" (with the s) is permanent. It never changes.

## 2. THE GAMES

**🌊 SPLASH — our flagship game.** Two teams, two pools, one rule: grab your rivals, drag them across the field, and dunk them in your pool. Last team standing wins. Fast, silly, instantly understandable — this is the game we promote everywhere.

**🔥 2D Battle** — the classic. 20 stages of strategic fire-baby combat with bronze knight armor progression.

**🌋 3D Arena** — full 3D multiplayer battles with Blaze and the crew: Story, Rescue, Survival, Free Roam, Practice, Tournaments, worldwide Leaderboards, 250 world tasks, and the silver armor track.

**🗺️ Open World** — a Godot-powered explorable island: 5 kingdoms, a volcano, and the hidden Heavenly and Underworld Kingdoms.

## 3. THE 6 PILLARS OF POWER

These are the project's constitution. Everything we ship must honor all six:

1. **Synthesized Web Audio** — all sound generated in code. No audio files, no licensing headaches.
2. **Toyetic look, zero exceptions** — plushie-style flame babies. NEVER beaks, wings, feathers, realistic humans, or anything resembling Angry Birds.
3. **Silent slapstick shorts** — 15–30 second vertical videos, no dialogue, pure physical comedy. The marketing engine.
4. **NFPA matchup engine** — real fire classes (A/B/C/D) matched against the right extinguisher types. Genuine fire-safety education inside the game.
5. **Lean packaging** — frozen baselines, small file targets. Every kilobyte must justify itself.
6. **Fire Safety Academy** — USFA/NFPA curriculum woven into the game and content. If it doesn't teach safety, it doesn't ship.

## 4. HARD BRAND RULES

- **NEVER** show Fire Babys burning or destroying homes. Promo clips show real game systems only.
- **NEVER** celebrate fire destroying buildings. In-game objectives say CAPTURE, never burn.
- Kid-safe everything: no profanity, no scary content.
- Free tools only. Nothing is spent without the owner's explicit approval.
- Nothing publishes or goes live without the owner's word.

## 6. FOR ADMINISTRATORS & MODERATORS

### What admins and mods DO
- Keep the community welcoming, kind, and kid-safe.
- Remove spam, bullying, and anything that breaks the hard brand rules.
- Help players who are stuck, confused, or reporting a problem.
- Draft announcements, events, and content for the owner to approve.

### What admins and mods NEVER do
- Delete programs, game names, player numbers, or customer/player profiles. Ever.
- Publish anything live (posts, game updates, announcements) without the owner's approval.
- Share anyone's personal information — players' or each other's.
- Make promises about release dates, features, or partnerships.

### Chain of command
The owner has the final word on everything. When in doubt, draft it and ask. Reporting a problem upward is always the right call — silence never is.
