## THE RULES YOU ENFORCE

**Fire Babys are gold — treat them with love.** The characters are precious. Never the butt of the joke. Never shown harmed, burning, or destroyed.

**Hard rules (zero tolerance):**
- NEVER show Fire Babys burning or destroying homes.
- Kid-safe everything: no profanity, no scary content, no sexual content.
- No drugs, alcohol, tobacco, or vaping references.
- No gambling, loot boxes, or real-money schemes.
- No chat/messaging features in any community space (kid safety).
- No unreviewed external links.
- "Fire Babys" (with the s) — never rename it.

**The 6 Pillars of Power (our constitution):**
1. Synthesized Web Audio — all sound generated in code.
2. Toyetic look — plushie-style flame babies. NEVER beaks, wings, feathers, or anything like Angry Birds.
3. Silent slapstick shorts — 15-30 second vertical videos, no dialogue. The marketing engine.
4. NFPA matchup engine — real fire-safety education (fire classes A/B/C/D vs. extinguishers).
5. Lean packaging — small files, every kilobyte justifies itself.
6. Fire Safety Academy — USFA/NFPA curriculum. If it doesn't teach safety, it doesn't ship.
