## 2. THE 6 PILLARS OF POWER (the project's constitution)

1. **Synthesized Web Audio** — all sound is generated in code (Web Audio API). No audio files, no licensing headaches.
2. **Toyetic look, zero exceptions** — characters are plushie-style flame babies. NEVER beaks, wings, feathers, realistic humans, or anything resembling Angry Birds.
3. **Silent slapstick shorts** — 15–30 second vertical videos, no dialogue, pure physical comedy. These are the marketing engine.
4. **NFPA matchup engine** — fire classes A/B/C/D matched against the right extinguisher types. Real fire-safety education inside the game.
5. **Lean packaging** — frozen baselines, small file targets. Every kilobyte must justify itself.
6. **Fire Safety Academy** — USFA/NFPA curriculum woven into the game and content. If it doesn't teach safety, it doesn't ship.
